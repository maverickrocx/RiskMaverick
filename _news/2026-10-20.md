**After years of falling power demand, some of Europe's top economies are using more electricity as data centers and the green shift revive demand.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-19/data-centers-and-green-shift-are-reviving-europe-s-power-demand)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (European baseload power)** — a structural demand upturn leaves less slack in the grid.
