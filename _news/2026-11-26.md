## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Balance of Power.*

**The Houthis are pushing toward Aden after taking much of Yemen's western coast, and have struck Saudi Arabia's financial district and energy ports.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-11-25/houthi-gains-in-yemen-expand-the-gulf-s-second-front-as-saudi-arabia-attacked) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-25/houthis-hit-saudi-finance-center-extending-tit-for-tat-attacks)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Red Sea freight and Brent volatility)** — control of Aden would put both the Gulf of Aden and Bab el-Mandeb under Houthi reach, a chokepoint risk the surplus narrative is not pricing.

**A record three-quarters of the global fleet of large product tankers are now carrying crude, according to Signal Ocean data, keeping the cost of shipping refined fuels high.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-25/most-large-fuel-tankers-now-ship-dirty-crude-upending-trading)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (clean tanker freight)** — vessels need costly cleaning to return to fuels, so product freight stays sticky and widens delivered-cost basis for jet and naphtha.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Some LNG tankers are diverting from Asia to Europe after European prices hit a four-year high, though a jump in Japanese spot power prices has made LNG-fired generation affordable there for the first time since February.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-25/europe-is-beating-asia-in-fight-for-gas-supply-disrupted-by-war) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-23/japan-power-prices-four-year-high-cold-outlook-winter-snow-weather-gas-lng)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (JKM–TTF spread volatility)** — a cold turn in Asia could reverse the cargo flow quickly, leaving Europe's spot-dependent winter cover exposed.
