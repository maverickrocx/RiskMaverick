*Sourced from this morning's Bloomberg and Economist newsletters.*

**US payrolls unexpectedly fell 23,000 in July, and the market read it as
good news.** The Bureau of Labor Statistics also revised hiring for the
prior two months lower, and wage growth slowed; the unemployment rate fell
even as headcount shrank, with much of the loss concentrated in state and
local government and leisure and hospitality. Bad news for workers proved
to be good news for stocks: the S&P 500 booked its best week since April as
traders priced in a lower chance the Fed raises rates at its September
meeting.
*Risk lens:* a weak print that rallies equities and dampens hike odds is
priced as unambiguously good — but it's the same "soft landing or
something worse" ambiguity that has whipsawed markets all year. A single
month of state-and-local-government layoffs is thin evidence either way.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/live-blog/2026-08-07/us-employment-report-for-july-live-updates) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/us-employers-unexpectedly-shed-jobs-unemployment-rate-falls)

**Trump is trying once more to remove Fed Governor Lisa Cook.** The White
House has reportedly told Cook it is "considering" removing her and asked
her to respond to earlier allegations of mortgage fraud, which she denies.
The Supreme Court suspended Trump's first attempt to fire her in June.
*Risk lens:* this runs alongside the Warsh-independence questions already
in the market — a second, separate front against the Fed's board
composition. A book hedged for "one governor under pressure" isn't hedged
for a pattern of them.
— [Economist](https://www.economist.com/united-states/2025/08/29/lisa-cook-the-fed-governor-donald-trump-is-trying-to-sack)

**BofA's sentiment gauge just hit its most extreme bullish reading since
2021 — its own signal to start cutting risk.** The bank's typical
contrarian sell signal has been triggered as investor positioning has
stretched further into the soft-data, rate-cut-hopes rally.
*Risk lens:* stretched positioning into a rally driven by weak data is a
setup where the reversal, if the data or the Fed disappoints, tends to be
sharper than the rally that preceded it — worth checking how much of a
portfolio's recent gains are riding on that positioning rather than on
fundamentals.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/bofa-sentiment-gauge-hits-most-extreme-bullish-level-since-2021)
