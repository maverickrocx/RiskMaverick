## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Markets Daily and Energy Daily.*

**Brent fell 2% to $96.40 a barrel on an unconfirmed report that Iran had eased its demands over the Strait of Hormuz as diplomacy to end the war continues.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/oil-falls-on-unconfirmed-report-that-iran-eased-hormuz-demands)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (front-month Brent)** — the drop rests on an unconfirmed report, so a denial would reverse it fast.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Europe began winter with gas storage under 75% full, and BloombergNEF's base case, with Hormuz shut to LNG until end-March, has inventories ending winter at just 17%.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-11-09/europe-s-gas-storage-problems-seen-lasting-well-beyond-winter)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (front-month TTF)** — a record-low end-winter buffer leaves prices exposed to any cold snap or Hormuz setback.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**China's weakening domestic gas prices are curbing its appetite for expensive seaborne LNG as winter nears, potentially freeing cargoes for Europe and other Asian buyers.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/china-s-cheap-domestic-gas-curbs-lng-buying-as-winter-nears)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (spot JKM)** — softer Chinese buying eases competition for cargoes just as peak demand arrives.

## Carbon {#carbon}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**The EU and UK are in the final stages of a deal to link their carbon markets, to be announced at a summit later this month, which would let permits trade across both systems and cut carbon-border-tax costs.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-08/eu-close-to-deal-to-link-uk-carbon-market-after-brexit-split)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (UKA–EUA convergence)** — linkage points to a narrower spread and deeper liquidity, with the deal still unannounced.
