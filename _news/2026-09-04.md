## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Evening Briefing Americas.*

**Oil advanced as Iran claimed fresh retaliatory strikes and Israel signalled it was ready to widen its role, with the six-month US–Iran conflict now described by both sides as stalled with no end in sight.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-sept-3) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/-we-are-stalled-us-iran-conflict-stuck-with-no-end-in-sight)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (front-month Brent)** — a frozen conflict with no de-escalation path keeps the Hormuz war premium embedded in the prompt contract indefinitely.

**US retail diesel climbed to its highest since mid-2022, and a cargo of South Korean diesel is making a rare 12,000-mile voyage to Europe as Ukrainian strikes on Russian refineries drain global supply.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/europe-s-diesel-crunch-spurs-rare-12-000-mile-journey-from-korea)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (distillate cracks)** — an Asia-to-Europe arbitrage that normally makes no economic sense is a hard signal of how tight physical middle distillates have become.

**Chevron, Eni and GE Vernova joined US Energy Secretary Chris Wright and Venezuela's acting president to unveil a wave of deals to lift the country's oil output.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/chevron-eni-lead-wave-of-deals-to-boost-venezuela-oil-output)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (near-term Brent balance)** — the incremental heavy-sour barrels are years away and matter for 2027-plus supply, not prompt pricing while Hormuz risk dominates.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**European gas storage is at its lowest for the time of year in data going back to 2009 — German tanks are just over half full — and European gas futures are at a three-year high heading into winter.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/energy-prices-surge-as-politicians-sound-alarm-ahead-of-winter) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/european-gas-futures-rise-to-highest-since-2023-on-iran-war)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (winter TTF)** — a thin storage cushion competing with Asia for LNG leaves the market no margin for a cold snap or a supply hiccup.

**Sweden's plan to build 10 new reactors by 2045 has become a central issue in its 13 September election, with the vote threatening to slow or reshape the programme.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/sweden-s-grand-nuclear-ambitions-come-down-to-an-election)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (Nordic baseload, long-dated)** — a policy-reversal risk to 2030s capacity with no bearing on the current power balance.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Bangladesh paid the most for LNG in about four years to curb power blackouts, while Pakistan scrapped an emergency tender after receiving a single very expensive offer — the first buyers priced out as Europe competes for cargoes.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/bangladesh-pays-most-for-gas-since-2022-to-ease-power-blackouts) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/pakistan-faces-further-blackouts-after-rejecting-pricey-lng)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Asian spot JKM)** — European restocking is setting the clearing price and marginal South Asian demand is being destroyed at it, tightening the seaborne market further.
