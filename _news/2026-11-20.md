## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from The Economic Times' ET Markets Morning, The Economist's World in Brief and Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Brent rose 24 cents to $92.22 early Friday after Houthi missiles hit Riyadh, but is still on track for a 3.5% weekly fall as Hormuz crude flows return to near prewar levels.** — [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/commodities/news/oil-prices-edge-higher-as-houthi-attacks-on-riyadh-raise-supply-concerns/articleshow/136010331.cms) · [The Economist](https://www.economist.com/middle-east-and-africa/2026/11/01/how-the-houthis-built-their-war-machine)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (front-month Brent)** — Saudi-targeted strikes add upside skew, but the surplus narrative still owns the trend; buy cheap calls rather than cut shorts.

**China's crude imports have climbed back to about 10 million barrels a day from just over 7 million in June, with analysts saying Brent below $80 would unlock heavier stockpiling.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/china-oil-imports-rebound-as-beijing-turns-back-to-stockpiling) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-11-19/china-oil-demand-rebound-pivots-on-two-crucial-factors-stockpiling-and-exports)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (deferred Brent spreads)** — price-sensitive Chinese buying near $80 acts as a soft floor on the back of the curve, limiting how far contango can deepen.

**US retail gasoline has fallen to an eight-month low because refiners are running flat out to make record-priced diesel, Bloomberg Opinion's Javier Blas writes.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/articles/2026-11-19/gas-prices-are-falling-as-a-side-effect-of-the-diesel-shortage)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (RBOB cracks)** — max-distillate runs keep spilling gasoline into the market, so the diesel-gasoline spread stays wide.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg Green.*

**The EU reached a tentative deal to delay methane rules for oil and gas importers by two years, double the reprieve its regulator had proposed, citing energy security after the Iran war.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/eu-reaches-deal-on-longer-delay-to-methane-rules-for-importers)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (TTF curve 2027)** — removing a compliance hurdle keeps US and other cargoes eligible for Europe, easing a supply risk priced into the strip.

**Global electricity demand for cooling buildings is overtaking demand for heating for the first time, the IEA said.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/global-cooling-demand-overtakes-need-for-heating-iea)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (summer peak power)** — load shifting to summer raises the value of summer peak hedges relative to winter baseload.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**As few as two LNG tankers appear to have left the Strait of Hormuz in the week through Tuesday, against a weekly average of eight over the previous two months, after a run of attacks.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/lng-exports-through-hormuz-appear-to-slow-after-more-attacks)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (prompt JKM)** — losing roughly three-quarters of Gulf outbound LNG voyages tightens December-January Asian cargoes just as heating demand peaks.

## Carbon {#carbon}

*Sourced from Bloomberg Green and The Economist's World in Brief.*

**The COP31 draft text dropped its call to phase out fossil fuels and instead backs electrification, around a voluntary pledge to lift electricity to 35% of final energy demand by 2035.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/climate-talks-edge-toward-deal-on-electrifying-global-economy) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-11-19/cop31-talks-retreat-on-plans-to-spur-retreat-from-fossil-fuels)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (EUA Dec-26)** — a voluntary global pledge does not change EU cap supply; compliance carbon prices take nothing from the text.

**Germany, the EU, the UK, France and Nigeria backed a pledge to curb SF6, a grid gas that traps about 24,000 times more heat than CO2 over a century.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/nations-take-aim-at-a-climate-threat-hiding-in-electricity-grids)
