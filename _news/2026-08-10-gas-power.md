*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Another wave of intense heat is sweeping an already parched Europe.** The
region's fifth of the summer raises wildfire risk, threatens crops, and dries
the watersheds feeding rivers that are already critically low.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/europe-s-fifth-heat-wave-risks-straining-rivers-power-and-crops)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (prompt power)** — low rivers hit power supply twice
over: hydro output falls and thermal plants lose cooling water just as
air-conditioning load peaks. That is a correlated squeeze, and it lands
alongside a gas-storage position already behind schedule. Shape and spark
spreads reprice before flat price does.
