*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Adnoc Gas is exploring an LNG export plant that would sit outside the Strait
of Hormuz entirely.** The unit of the UAE's biggest oil producer is weighing a
new liquefaction facility on the country's east coast, placing loadings beyond
the contested waterway. The UAE has moved more crude through Hormuz than any
other producer over the past two months, and has had vessels struck in
transit — including one targeted by a missile at the weekend.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/adnoc-gas-exploring-new-lng-export-plant-to-avoid-hormuz-strait) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/adnoc-says-three-vessels-hit-this-week-while-transiting-hormuz)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (long-dated Hormuz risk premium)** — capital being
committed to bypass infrastructure is a signal that producers are treating the
disruption as structural rather than temporary. It is bearish for the
chokepoint premium at the back of the curve and bullish for it at the front:
the relief only arrives years out, and everything before first cargo still
transits the strait.
