## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Evening Briefing Asia and Energy Daily, and The Economic Times.*

**Brent fell below $106 a barrel after rising more than 7% over the previous two days, with WTI near $93, as US and Iranian negotiators explored a phased deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-24/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-date)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (Brent volatility)** — two-day moves of 7% both ways are now routine. Size stops and margin buffers for headline-driven gaps, not trend.

**Iraq is offering buyers last-minute crude cargoes as record freight rates make shipments from deep inside the Gulf harder to place.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-25/iraq-offers-last-minute-oil-cargoes-as-record-freight-costs-bite)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (Basrah OSP differentials)** — freight, not crude, is setting delivered cost. Watch basis between Gulf grades and Atlantic Basin barrels widen.

**India, now roughly 10% of global seaborne diesel supply, will keep exporting even if the US bans overseas sales, Oil Minister Hardeep Puri said.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-24/india-to-keep-up-diesel-exports-as-us-ban-prospect-roils-market) · [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/energy/oil-gas/india-to-continue-diesel-exports-despite-us-ban-threat-says-hardeep-singh-puri-amid-global-fuel-crunch/articleshow/134475630.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (European gasoil cracks)** — Indian barrels would partly backfill any US withdrawal. That caps, but does not remove, the export-ban tail.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**The Netherlands wants mandatory gas-storage targets scrapped under the EU's security-of-supply revamp, after spending almost €1 billion this year filling its reserves.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-25/dutch-seek-end-to-gas-storage-targets-after-1-billion-bill)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (TTF summer–winter spread)** — removing fill mandates weakens forced summer buying. The seasonal spread would carry less structural support.
