## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from the Economic Times.*

**Iran's Supreme National Security Council declared the Strait of Hormuz closed and said it will not reopen until Washington changes course, even as the US said its Navy escorted more than 15 million barrels of oil through the strait in a single day this week.** The dueling claims came after President Trump described the waterway as "American territory" and launched an "Economic D-Day" sanctions campaign targeting Iran's oil-smuggling networks.
— [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/iran-says-strait-of-hormuz-will-remain-closed-until-us-changes-course/articleshow/133433203.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Gulf war-risk premium)** — dueling claims over control of the world's busiest oil chokepoint keep freight and war-risk insurance elevated regardless of the actual flow of barrels.

**India's crude imports from Venezuela jumped to about 444,000 barrels a day in August, overtaking Iraq and the US, as refiners diversify away from Middle Eastern supply routes exposed to the Hormuz standoff.** Middle Eastern crude's share of India's imports has fallen to roughly 30% from 43% a year earlier, even as Russia still supplies more than half of India's barrels.
— [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/energy/oil-gas/india-reworks-oil-map-with-venezuela-as-hormuz-battlefield-keeps-gulf-flows-in-doubt/articleshow/133371249.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (hedge effectiveness)** — rerouting to Latin America reduces single-chokepoint exposure, but longer voyages raise freight and insurance costs that partly erode the hedge.

**Russia is importing roughly 270,000 tons of refined fuel from Asia this month via ship-to-ship transfers designed to mask cargo origins, as sustained Ukrainian strikes on its refineries force a rare pivot from net exporter to importer.** About a third of the cargo originated in India, with the rest loaded off South Korea and Malaysia.
— [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/energy/oil-gas/russia-imports-270000-tons-of-refined-fuel-from-asia-amid-ukraine-conflict/articleshow/133343968.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (product cracks)** — a major refiner turning importer after sustained attacks signals tightening diesel and gasoline supply that should support product cracks near-term.
