## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Markets Daily.*

**Brent fell below $100 a barrel for the first time since early October after Trump played down the prospect of strikes on Iran following the midterm rout.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-04/trump-plays-down-prospect-of-strikes-on-iran-after-midterm-rout)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (front-month Brent)** — the geopolitical premium is bleeding out, but a renewed Iran flare-up could reprice it quickly.

**The UAE is weighing a third crude export pipeline that bypasses the Strait of Hormuz, according to people familiar with the matter.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-04/uae-mulls-third-crude-oil-pipeline-to-avoid-strait-of-hormuz)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (Hormuz risk premium, long-dated)** — more bypass capacity trims chokepoint exposure over time, though nothing changes for flows near term.

**Oil has stopped moving in lockstep with Treasury yields: since the Sept. 15 peak in the correlation, higher crude has increasingly come with lower yields as growth fears displace inflation.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-04/oil-loses-its-grip-on-bonds-as-growth-fears-displace-inflation)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (cross-asset hedges)** — oil-versus-rates hedges built on the old correlation are now less reliable.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Democrats are set to take control of Georgia's utility regulator for the first time in decades, powered by voter anger over rising electricity bills.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-04/democrats-ride-anger-over-power-bills-to-take-over-georgia-board)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (US data-centre power demand growth)** — tougher rate and approval scrutiny slows load growth assumptions in a key hub.

**India has asked miners and railroads to help triple coal stockpiles at power stations before next summer after a supply crunch caused blackouts.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-04/india-pushes-to-triple-coal-stockpiles-to-avert-summer-blackouts)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Asian thermal coal)** — a stockpiling push adds a firm bid under seaborne coal and competing fuels.
