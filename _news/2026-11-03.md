## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Points of Return and Energy Daily.*

**Brent tumbled 11.4% on Monday, its biggest drop in six months, after Iran's Supreme Leader empowered the government to hold talks on the war.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-02/iran-s-khamenei-gives-government-powers-to-hold-talks-on-war)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (front-month Brent)** — the war premium is unwinding on talk, not on flows, so expect sharp two-way volatility until Tuesday's midterm outcome and Iran's follow-through are clear.

**Goldman Sachs raised its year-end and 2027 oil price forecasts, citing elevated supply risks after months of war in the Middle East.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-02/goldman-hikes-oil-price-forecasts-on-substantial-supply-risks)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (deferred Brent)** — bank forecast upgrades support the back end of the curve even as the front month sells off.
