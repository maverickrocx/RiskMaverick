## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Morning Briefing Asia.*

**Oil edged higher in thin holiday trading after a Houthi attack on a Saudi refinery threatened the recovery in Middle East crude exports.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-25/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-nov-26)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Brent front-month volatility)** — with US desks shut, thin liquidity magnifies any headline from the Red Sea; widen stops rather than add size.

**OPEC+ is considering rolling its current output quotas over into January as it contends with uncertainty from the Iran war, two delegates said.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-26/opec-considers-sticking-with-current-oil-quotas-delegates-say)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (Brent calendar spreads)** — a rollover removes a supply surprise from the December meeting, leaving spreads driven by Gulf disruption risk alone.

**European diesel futures have fallen almost 30% from their peak early in the US-Iran war, with a marked drop this month.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-26/diesel-s-slump-offers-brief-respite-from-global-energy-turmoil)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (ICE gasoil cracks)** — the slide compresses refining margins into winter; hedgers who locked crack spreads near the peak hold the better side of the basis.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Qatar's LNG exports have climbed to their highest since the US-Iran war began in February, adding supply to Europe and Asia just as peak winter demand starts.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-26/qatar-has-boosted-lng-exports-to-their-highest-since-february)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (prompt JKM and TTF)** — more Qatari cargoes ease the scramble for spot supply, though Hormuz transit keeps the chokepoint premium alive.
