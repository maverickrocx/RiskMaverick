## ⚡ Energy — oil

**Crude extends a fifth straight advance as the Hormuz deal wobbles.** WTI settled near
**$83.20/bbl** (+1.3%) and Brent near **$88.91/bbl** (+1.4%) this week after a senior
Iranian official said the Strait would not reopen until Washington unfreezes Iranian
funds and pays reparations — hardening a stance Tehran and Oman had described as
"close" to a deal days earlier.
*Risk lens:* every headline out of the Oman channel is now a standalone volatility
event; option markets are pricing binary deal/no-deal risk into a chokepoint that
normally carries a fifth of global seaborne crude.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/oil-prices-today-us-crude-84.html) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-hormuz-trump-iran.html)

**IEA: the 2026 supply shortfall is deepening, not closing.** The agency's August Oil
Market Report projects global supply will fall **4.3 mb/d** this year and puts the
Q3 deficit at **1.8 mb/d**, citing the Hormuz shutdown, the US blockade on Iranian
exports, Bab el-Mandeb attacks and lower Kazakh CPC volumes.
*Risk lens:* a widening physical deficit on top of thin inventories raises the odds of
a disorderly price spike on any further chokepoint escalation.
— [IEA](https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-august-2026)

**Product cracks, not crude, are where the real stress sits.** The NYMEX 3-2-1 crack
spread recently pushed to an all-time high near **$72/bbl** as diesel and jet-fuel
markets stay chronically tight — Moscow's suspension of diesel exports and refinery
outages in Australia and Texas have compounded the Hormuz-driven crude squeeze.
*Risk lens:* refiners are earning record margins while diesel and jet stay sticky even
if crude eases — a basis risk a flat-price crude hedge alone won't cover.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/turning-oil-to-fuel-has-never-been-more-profitable-as-refining-margins-surge) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/garthfriesen/2026/07/23/refining-stocks-soar-as-crack-spread-hits-record-high-in-2026/)

## 🔥 Gas & LNG

**TTF grinds higher on the same Hormuz doubts.** European front-month gas rose toward
**€60/MWh** this week as traders reassessed whether Qatari LNG cargoes can keep moving
freely while the Strait talks stay unresolved.
*Risk lens:* the winter curve is exposed to any further slippage in the Hormuz
negotiations, and a US-hub hedge does nothing to cover it.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/european-gas-rises-as-hormuz-deal-prospects-remain-murky)

**Henry Hub stays anchored below $3.** The EIA's latest Short-Term Energy Outlook trims
its Q3 2026 Henry Hub forecast to **$2.87/MMBtu**, 50 cents below last month's call,
citing record US production and comfortable domestic storage.
*Risk lens:* textbook basis divergence — TTF is pricing chokepoint risk that Henry Hub
barely reflects.
— [EIA](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/report/natgas.php)

## 🪙 Metals

**Copper holds near record highs as Codelco's flagship mine stays shut.** LME copper is
holding near **$14,000/tonne**, its highest level on record, after Codelco halted
expansion work at El Teniente — the world's largest underground copper mine — over a
newly identified seismic risk; a union leader said the pause could run up to two years.
*Risk lens:* a structural supply hit layered on a demand story (electrification, AI
data-centre build-out) argues for treating this rally as more than a speculative spike
when sizing hedges.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/copper-heads-for-record-close-in-london-on-tighter-global-market) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/copper-holds-14-000-as-weaker-us-rate-outlook-boosts-metals) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/codelco-halts-expansion-project-at-top-mine-on-new-seismic-risk)

**Gold holds above $4,400 as tame CPI eases hike fears.** Bullion extended gains after
Wednesday's in-line July inflation print, even as rising oil argued the other way;
China's central bank added roughly **20 tons** to reserves in July, its 21st straight
month of buying.
*Risk lens:* gold is trading two competing narratives at once — disinflation support
and energy-driven inflation risk — so its correlation with rates can flip fast.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/gold-steadies-as-traders-watch-us-inflation-for-rate-hike-clues) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/china-s-central-bank-adds-20-tons-to-gold-reserves-in-july)

## 📊 Macro & cross-asset

**July CPI cools to 3.4%, but the Fed's September call stays a coin flip.** Headline
CPI rose **0.1%** on the month (3.4% annual), core CPI **0.2%** (2.5% annual) — in line
with estimates. Odds of a 25bp September hike have swung sharply over the past week as
a soft July jobs report and now a benign CPI print pull against the earlier hawkish
drift from rising energy prices.
*Risk lens:* an inflation print this close to consensus should be a non-event, but it's
landing on top of an energy-driven cost shock — the read-through to rates and FX books
is unusually noisy right now.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/cpi-inflation-report-july-2026.html) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/07/odds-the-fed-hikes-in-september-tumble-following-big-july-jobs-miss.html)

**Equities grind to fresh highs on the CPI relief.** The S&P 500 added **0.26%** to
**7,748.50**, the Nasdaq Composite gained **0.54%** to **26,588.49**, while the Dow
slipped **0.04%** to **53,770.27** as megacap chip names led the advance.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/stock-market-today-dow-s-p-live-updates)
