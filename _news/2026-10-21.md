## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Markets Daily.*

**Brent rose to a five-month high above $111 a barrel, trading at $111.59, up 1.9%, as traders weighed the threat of Iranian strikes on energy flows.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-19/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-oct-20)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (crude tanker freight)** — little new compliant tonnage means freight stays tight.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**PJM Interconnection, the largest US grid operator, declared a level-one emergency alert at the start of last week, and its operations head says the margin for error is shrinking.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-10-20/us-power-grid-strains-become-a-year-round-problem)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (JKM and front-month TTF)** — attacks on tankers have removed the recovery hope that had capped prices.
