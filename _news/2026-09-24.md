## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Evening Briefing Americas and Morning Briefing Asia.*

**The WTI cash roll at Cushing surged above $5 a barrel for the first time in data going back to 2017, as crude closed almost 4% higher at around $103.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-09-23/us-debt-selloff-spans-most-maturities-evening-briefing-americas)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (prompt WTI spreads)** — record backwardation at the delivery hub makes rolling short-dated hedges forward expensive. Physical tightness, not futures positioning, is setting the prompt.

**The White House denied a report that it was preparing a 90-day ban on US diesel exports, with the energy secretary saying the administration is instead working with refiners on voluntary curbs.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-23/us-prepares-plan-for-90-day-diesel-export-ban-politico-reports)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (ULSD futures)** — denial without a firm ruling-out keeps policy headline risk alive. Expect elevated volatility in diesel cracks until the export question is settled.

**Iran's President Masoud Pezeshkian told the UN General Assembly that Tehran won't allow freedom of navigation through the Strait of Hormuz while sanctions and a US blockade remain.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-23/iran-president-defends-country-s-right-to-nuclear-enrichment)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Brent war-risk premium)** — this undercuts the reading of progress in US-Iran talks. Chokepoint exposure stays priced in for longer.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Evening Briefing Americas.*

**LNG deliveries into mainland China more than doubled last week to the highest in almost two years, according to BloombergNEF, with Russia the largest supplier ahead of Australia and Malaysia.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-09-23/a-us-diesel-export-ban-would-be-a-calamity-for-the-market)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (prompt JKM)** — Chinese storage restocking competes directly for spot Pacific cargoes. Watch the JKM–TTF basis widen if the buying persists.

**Shell and its partners are poised to approve doubling the LNG Canada export project.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-23/shell-partners-poised-to-approve-doubling-of-lng-canada-project)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (back-end JKM)** — added Pacific supply later in the decade weighs on the long-dated curve, not the prompt.
