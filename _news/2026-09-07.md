## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Iran and the US carried out what appeared to be their largest tit-for-tat tanker strikes yet over the weekend, an escalation that pushed oil prices higher.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-06/iran-says-it-targeted-oil-tankers-in-response-to-us-strikes)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (front-month crude)** — a fresh round of tit-for-tat tanker strikes reloads the war-risk premium just as flows had shown signs of stabilizing.

**High energy prices — with gasoline and diesel up almost 40% since the Strait of Hormuz closed — helped Germany's Alternative for Germany score its best-ever state election result in Saxony-Anhalt.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-09-07/high-energy-costs-helped-a-german-far-right-party-to-victory)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (European energy-policy risk)** — a populist party campaigning to end Russia sanctions and extend coal use gaining ground raises the odds of policy volatility around EU energy rules.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Qatari LNG tankers are heading back toward the Persian Gulf, a sign the supplier may be positioning vessels for a resumption of exports through the Strait of Hormuz.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-07/qatar-is-bringing-empty-lng-ships-home-in-possible-export-pivot)

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Green Daily.*

**Spain's renewables lead — now about 60% of grid electricity — is reaching its limits without a "battery revolution," with installed storage at less than 2% of its 2030 target of 22.5 gigawatts.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-09-06/spain-s-renewable-energy-lead-is-at-risk-without-a-battery-revolution)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (grid-stability risk)** — a storage shortfall alongside a solar glut leaves the grid exposed to the kind of frequency swings behind last year's Iberian blackout.
