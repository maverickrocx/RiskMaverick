## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Two oil supertankers — one Saudi-operated, one linked to the secretive Sinokor shuttle trade — were struck by projectiles transiting the Strait of Hormuz, imperiling a recovery that had restored flows to roughly half prewar levels.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/two-oil-supertankers-hit-by-projectiles-in-hormuz-marisks-says)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Hormuz war-risk premium)** — renewed strikes on transiting tankers threaten the shuttle-trade flows that have kept a lid on crude prices.

**Trump is framing his deal for a US stake in Venezuela's oil reserves as a win that will lower gasoline prices and replenish depleted stockpiles, but Bloomberg says it's unclear the deal will do either.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/trump-s-venezuela-deal-leaves-100-billion-question-unanswered)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (US reserve rebuild)** — the deal's political framing outruns its near-term supply case, leaving the price-relief story unproven.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Russia has almost doubled the "dark fleet" of tankers ferrying LNG from its US-sanctioned Arctic project in under a year, pushing exports higher despite Western restrictions.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/russia-doubles-its-lng-dark-fleet-in-race-to-boost-gas-exports)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (sanctions enforcement)** — a fast-growing shadow fleet undercuts the supply squeeze Western sanctions on Russian LNG were meant to deliver.

## Carbon {#carbon}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Solar panels overtook coal as China's top source of power capacity, a milestone BloombergNEF called a marker of the country's green-energy boom.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/solar-surpasses-coal-as-china-s-top-source-of-power-capacity)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (thermal-coal demand)** — the milestone signals a structural shift in China's generation mix that thins long-run coal and carbon-intensive baseload demand.
