## ⚡ Energy — oil

**OPEC+ finishes unwinding its 2023 cuts, then signals a pause.** The group
ratified a further **188,000 bpd** increase for September at its virtual
meeting on 2 August, completing the phased rollback of the **1.65 million
bpd** cut originally agreed in 2023. Delegates said OPEC+ will pause further
hikes in the fourth quarter, leaving roughly **2 million bpd** of the
separate 2022 cuts intact, with harder talks looming over 2027 production
baselines.
*Risk lens:* the pause removes a source of forward supply growth just as
Hormuz risk is rising again — the two shocks compound rather than offset.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-02/opec-makes-small-quota-hike-to-finish-unwinding-2023-oil-cuts) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/08/02/opec-agrees-september-oil-hike-completing-rollback-of-voluntary-cuts.html)

**A second LNG tanker is hit transiting Hormuz.** The Gaslog Shanghai,
carrying Qatari-origin cargo, was struck by an unidentified projectile in
the strait's southern corridor on 1 August, knocking out power before the
resulting fire was put out; no crew were hurt. Qatar responded by
suspending maritime activity at its LNG terminals over the weekend.
*Risk lens:* an operator-confirmed strike on a chokepoint asset is a harder
signal than the disputed attacks earlier in the crisis — expect war-risk
insurance and freight to reprice again.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-01/tanker-carrying-qatari-lng-struck-while-transiting-hormuz)

## 🔥 Gas & LNG

**TTF reopens above €50 on the weekend escalation.** The front-month
contract jumped **3.35%** to **€50.43/MWh** (about $59.51) as of Monday
morning in Amsterdam, rebounding from Friday's decline after Qatar's halt
to maritime activity revived fears for LNG loadings out of the Gulf.
*Risk lens:* a single weekend headline erased Friday's entire pullback — in
this regime, weekend gap risk on gas books is as large as anything that
happens during the trading week.
— [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/european-natural-gas-prices-jump-074500768.html) · [OilPrice.com](https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/European-Natural-Gas-Prices-Jump-on-Hormuz-Escalation.amp.html)

## 📊 Macro & cross-asset

**The 30-year yield sits at a 19-year high.** Long-bond yields jumped as
much as 14bp to nearly **5.24%** immediately after Fed Chair Kevin Warsh
held rates on 29 July for a seventh straight month without offering
forward guidance; the market read the silence as a lack of resolve on
inflation and sold the long end hard.
*Risk lens:* a hold that pushes term premium higher is a curve-steepening
event — duration hedges calibrated to a dovish Fed are wrong-signed here.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/bond-rout-sends-warning-to-warsh-that-tough-talk-is-not-enough)

**The Nasdaq confirms its second correction of 2026.** The index closed 29
July at **24,442.94**, down **10.1%** from its 1 June high of 27,190.21, as
an AI-spending selloff in chip names compounded the Hormuz and rates
overhang.
*Risk lens:* three distinct risk factors — AI valuations, an energy shock
and rates — are now moving the same book at the same time, which is
exactly when historical correlations break down.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/nasdaq-100-heads-for-correction-as-ai-worries-rattle-investors)

**Equities staged a Friday rebound regardless.** The S&P 500 added **0.7%**
to 7,489.72, the Nasdaq Composite rose **1%** to 25,373.85, and the Dow
gained **0.53%** to 52,485.03 on 31 July, led by an Amazon earnings jump,
even as bond yields and oil kept climbing.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)
