## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**China's total fuel-export quotas for the year have reached about 42 million tons, the most since 2021, but its diesel-inventory gauge is at the lowest since 2022, so Beijing may hold back shipments.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-11-05/china-weighs-global-diesel-lifeline-against-tight-domestic-market)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (Asian diesel cracks)** — the quota is a call option on supply, and the split by product and monthly approvals will decide whether it eases the market.

**Brent rose from a two-month low after tumbling almost 9% in the prior session, as traders bet a Democratic sweep could speed an end to the Middle East war.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-04/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-nov-5)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (front-month Brent)** — peace hopes are driving the price, so a stalled ceasefire would snap the premium back fast.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Green Daily.*

**Woodside, Australia's largest gas producer, will cut about 11% of its workforce, more than 500 jobs, to target $400 million of annual savings from 2028.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-04/woodside-tweaks-dividend-policy-targets-400-million-in-savings)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (Australian LNG supply)** — an overhead cut, not a volume cut, so there is no direct effect on near-term cargoes.

**Solar and battery sales at a Dhaka market are up about 50% year on year as the Middle East gas crisis worsens Bangladesh's blackouts.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-05/solar-market-in-bangladesh-shows-shift-from-oil-gas-due-to-middle-east-war)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (long-dated Asian LNG demand)** — high-growth importers like Bangladesh are building alternatives that could weaken the demand outlook.
