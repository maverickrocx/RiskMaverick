## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (US LNG contracting pipeline)** — sustained Hormuz-supply entrapment is the structural tailwind pushing Asian and European buyers into long-term US offtake deals.
