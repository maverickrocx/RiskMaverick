## ⚡ Energy — oil

**Brent extends its climb as Hormuz reopening hopes fade.** Brent settled at
**$88.91/bbl** and WTI at **$83.20** on Tuesday, both up more than **6% on the
week**, after a senior Iranian security official said the strait will not
reopen until Washington unfreezes Iranian funds held overseas. Brent has now
advanced for six straight sessions, up roughly 12% since early last week, as
headlines swing between claims a deal is close and signs the talks have
stalled.
*Risk lens:* this is a headline-driven, binary market — price is moving on
claims that get made and then walked back within the same news cycle, a
different volatility regime from a normal supply/demand book and a reason to
be wary of selling optionality into the next round of talks.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/oil-prices-today-us-crude-84.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-aug-12)

**The US emergency buffer is now the thinnest since 1983.** Crude held in the
Strategic Petroleum Reserve fell by roughly 6.1 million barrels last week to
about 298.7 million — below 300 million for the first time in over four
decades — following the 172-million-barrel release Washington authorised in
March in response to the Hormuz disruption.
*Risk lens:* a depleted SPR is one less lever the government has to cushion a
genuine supply shock, which raises the tail risk sitting behind the current
stand-off even before flat price moves.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/oil-in-strategic-petroleum-reserve-falls-below-300-million-barrels-lowest-since-1983.html)

## 🔥 Gas & LNG

**European gas keeps re-pricing the same risk Henry Hub is ignoring.** TTF
climbed to **€61.8/MWh** on Tuesday, extending Monday's 8.3% jump to
**€60.20**, as the stalled Hormuz talks that are moving oil pushed European
buyers to bid up cargoes.
*Risk lens:* a textbook basis divergence — a book hedged on Henry Hub carries
none of this move; the exposure sits entirely in the TTF/JKM leg.
— [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/european-natural-gas-prices-jump-074500768.html)

**The EIA cut its Henry Hub forecast again.** The August Short-Term Energy
Outlook trimmed the Q3 2026 Henry Hub forecast to **$2.87/MMBtu** — down 50
cents from July's outlook — and now sees prices below $3/MMBtu through
November, with end-October storage projected at a record **3,985 Bcf**, 5%
above the five-year average.
— [EIA STEO](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/report/natgas.php)

## 🪙 Metals

**Copper is holding above $14,000/tonne after setting a fresh record.** US
Comex copper touched **$6.70/lb (~$14,780/t)** in early August, and London
prices remain above the prior mid-May record, after a surge of metal shipped
to US ports ahead of a possible tariff decision tightened availability
elsewhere, on top of structural demand from data centres and grid buildout.
*Risk lens:* a tariff-driven relocation of physical stock, not a demand
collapse, is behind the tightness — the US/ex-US spread is the position to
watch as much as the flat price.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/copper-holds-14-000-as-weaker-us-rate-outlook-boosts-metals) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/copper-market-crunch-brews-as-us-and-china-compete-for-metal)

**Gold sits at its highest in over two months, running into the CPI print.**
Spot gold traded near **$4,397/oz** on Tuesday, its third straight advance,
as markets positioned for Wednesday's inflation data with Fed policy for
September seen close to a coin flip.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/select/the-price-of-gold-today-august-11-2026/) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-prices-today-tuesday-august-11-2026-gold-remains-over-4400-as-iran-situation-worsens-115212228.html)

## 📊 Macro & cross-asset

**July's CPI split the difference — a soft headline, a hot core.** Headline
CPI rose **0.2% on the month (2.7% year-on-year)**, coming in slightly below
forecast, while core CPI rose **0.3% (3.1% year-on-year)** — above the 3.0%
consensus and its highest annual reading since February — as tariff-linked
increases in goods prices and a rebound in airfares and medical-care costs
offset continued cooling in shelter.
*Risk lens:* a report that reads as dovish on the headline and hawkish on the
core is easy to misprice in either direction; a book positioned off the
headline alone isn't hedged for the Fed weighing the core number more
heavily.
— [Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/economy/july-us-cpi-report-shows-inflation-27-annual-rate-slightly-below-expected) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/u-july-cpi-rose-softer-123639502.html)

**Equities sat just below record highs heading into the print.** The S&P 500
slipped 0.3% on Tuesday to close near 7,753, weighed down by Hormuz-linked
uncertainty, but stayed close to Friday's record closing high of 7,757.64,
set after July's weak jobs report.
— [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-tuesday-august-11-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-102827503.html)
