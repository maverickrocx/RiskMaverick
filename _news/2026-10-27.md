## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Europe's gas benchmark has more than tripled this year to about €97/MWh, with storage only about 75% full against a 90% seasonal average, and Energy Aspects sees a spike above €200 if winter is cold.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-10-26/surging-natural-gas-price-tests-europe-s-appetite-for-action)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (front-month TTF, volatility)** — thin storage cover makes weather the swing factor and leaves short-vol positions exposed.

**EQT agreed to buy Range Resources in an all-stock deal worth about $12.8 billion, creating what it calls the largest and only vertically integrated US natural gas producer.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-26/eqt-to-buy-range-resources-in-an-all-stock-transaction)
