## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Morning Briefing Americas and Asia, The Economist's Business in Brief and The Economic Times.*

**The US and Iran exchanged fire for the first time in about a month — US strikes on Iran's Larak Island in the Strait of Hormuz, Iranian missiles and drones at US bases in Jordan and the UAE — sending Brent back above $90 and WTI up almost 4%.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-30/us-strikes-iranian-rocket-launchers-in-first-attack-in-weeks) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-30/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-aug-31)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (front-month Brent)** — the war-risk premium reprices on every Hormuz exchange of fire, and a sustained return to fighting would keep the prompt contract bid through the autumn.

**Washington will take majority control of more than 65 billion barrels of Venezuela's proven reserves, with Caracas set to net about $19 a barrel and the crude earmarked to refill the depleted US emergency reserve.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-28/trump-says-us-secured-control-of-some-venezuelan-oil-reserves) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-30/trump-s-venezuela-oil-grab-reprises-industry-s-neocolonial-past) · [The Economist](https://www.economist.com/the-americas/2026/08/29/americas-murky-deal-to-secure-a-fifth-of-venezuelas-oil)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (near-term Brent balance)** — incremental Venezuelan barrels are quarters away and too small to move prompt balances, and the reserve-refill intent is a latent bid rather than a 2026 supply event.

**Indian Oil lifted LPG output by nearly 30% and ran its refineries above capacity as Hormuz disruption strained supply, with roughly 90% of India's LPG imports and 45% of its crude imports transiting the strait.**
— [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/energy/oil-gas/indian-oil-ramps-up-lpg-output-30-as-hormuz-disruption-tests-indias-energy-security/articleshow/133649054.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Asian LPG and naphtha)** — the world's third-largest crude consumer leaning on domestic output and alternative grades points to persistent import-parity stress while the strait stays constrained.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg.*

**California lawmakers blocked Governor Newsom's plan to shield PG&E, Edison International and Sempra from wildfire liabilities, opting for a bill that updates the state's wildfire response without moving liability off investor-owned utilities; PG&E fell 10% and Edison 4.6%.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-28/newsom-blocked-on-push-to-shield-utilities-from-fire-liabilities)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (California IOU credit and equity)** — leaving inverse-condemnation liability intact keeps wildfire tail-risk on utility balance sheets and pressures spreads as peak fire season arrives.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg and The Economic Times — the morning newsletters carried no LNG coverage.*

**Qatar extended its force majeure on LNG deliveries to European and Asian buyers by another month as Strait of Hormuz traffic stayed halted, with cargo cancellations to Pakistan now running into October and Asian spot LNG trading above $23.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-28/qatar-extends-lng-force-majeure-as-hormuz-traffic-remains-halted)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (JKM and TTF)** — a rolling monthly force majeure on the world's largest LNG supplier removes any quick supply-normalisation path and keeps a war premium embedded in winter contracts.

**India's LNG imports rose to 59% of gas consumption in July, the highest share since 2021, leaving buyers exposed to elevated spot prices that Equirus expects to climb further from September.**
— [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/energy/oil-gas/higher-lng-prices-likely-from-september-downstream-players-may-face-margin-pressure-costs-report/articleshow/133645411.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (Indian city-gas and downstream margins)** — record import dependence at high spot prices locks in negative operating leverage for gas distributors until Hormuz flows normalise.
