## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Markets Daily.*

**Brent rose above $110 a barrel after a state-run Iranian news outlet said a supertanker hit a sea mine in the Strait of Hormuz.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-20/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-oct-21)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (Brent implied volatility)** — binary diplomatic outcomes keep optionality expensive.
