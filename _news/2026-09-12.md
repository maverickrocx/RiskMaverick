## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Businessweek Daily.*

**The International Energy Agency again cut its global oil demand and supply forecasts, saying the war's disruption will now persist to at least year-end and that the crisis is "as grave as any of the biggest shocks" of the past six decades; Saudi Arabia separately told OPEC its output has slumped to its lowest level since the 1990 Gulf War.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/iea-warns-oil-demand-may-have-to-fall-further-as-iran-war-drags) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/saudis-tell-opec-that-output-slumped-again-to-lowest-since-1990)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (term structure)** — a forecaster that has already coordinated one emergency stockpile release is now flagging further demand destruction into year-end, which reads as a structural gap rather than a seasonal one.

**Saudi Arabia shut down its East-West pipeline — the kingdom's main route for moving crude to the Red Sea without transiting the Strait of Hormuz — as a precaution after multiple attacks this week.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/saudi-oil-pipeline-that-bypasses-hormuz-shut-after-attacks)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Brent, chokepoint premium)** — Petroline was the market's chief hedge against a full Hormuz closure; taking it offline too leaves exporters with materially fewer options if the strait itself shuts.

**US diesel prices topped $6 a gallon for the first time in history, with AAA putting the national average at $6.0556 — up about 62% from a year ago — as the Iran war and Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian refineries keep squeezing distillate supply.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/us-diesel-prices-rise-past-6-a-gallon-for-first-time-ever)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (distillate cracks)** — a national record, not a regional spike, confirms the crack-spread tightness central banks have started watching is a durable feature of the balance rather than noise.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**European natural gas prices are headed for their biggest weekly gain since July as the Middle East conflict intensified, deepening fears of longer disruptions to energy flows just as Europe works to refill storage before winter; prices have more than doubled since the war began.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/europe-gas-set-for-biggest-weekly-gain-since-july-as-war-expands)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (winter TTF)** — the war is now squeezing flows at exactly the point in the calendar when Europe most needs uncontested cargoes to finish the storage build.
