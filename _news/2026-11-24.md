## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**European gas jumped almost 9% as forecasts turned sharply colder for northwest Europe into early December, reversing earlier calls for a brief chill followed by mild weather.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-23/european-gas-surges-as-cold-snap-threatens-tight-inventories)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (front-month TTF)** — weather-driven withdrawals against already tight storage keep front-month volatility bid and steepen the winter-over-summer spread.
