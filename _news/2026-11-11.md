## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Brent crude slid below $90 a barrel for the first time since August as traders added to bets on a resolution of the Middle East war.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-nov-10)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (front-month Brent)** — the move prices an absence of fresh fighting rather than a settled peace, so headline risk on the upside stays live.

**Refined-product loadings inside the Persian Gulf rose to about 3 million barrels a day in October, per Vortexa, up from a May trough near 1 million but still roughly half pre-conflict levels.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/rising-fuel-flows-through-hormuz-may-help-ease-strained-market)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (East-of-Suez diesel cracks)** — returning Gulf barrels ease the tightest corner of the products market, narrowing regional arbitrage.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Qatar's LNG loadings have picked up over recent weeks to around half of pre-war levels despite continuing disruption in the Strait of Hormuz.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/qatar-lng-loadings-pick-up-to-around-half-of-pre-war-levels)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (prompt JKM)** — extra Qatari cargoes loosen winter supply, though flows remain hostage to Hormuz security.

## Carbon {#carbon}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Green Daily.*

**The Global Carbon Budget estimates fossil-fuel CO2 emissions will fall 0.4% in 2026 to 38.2 billion tonnes, driven mainly by a 1.9% drop in oil-related emissions after the Iran-war price shock.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/global-carbon-emissions-set-to-dip-in-2026-after-iran-war-oil-surge)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (EUA)** — a shock-driven, likely temporary dip says little about structural allowance demand.
