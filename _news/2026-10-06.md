## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Evening Briefing Americas, The Economist's Business in Brief, and The Economic Times.*

**Oil fell after Saudi Arabia cut its benchmark crude price for Asia to a six-year low as Persian Gulf flows recover, though Brent is still around $102 a barrel.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-04/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-oct-5) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-04/why-is-oil-still-almost-100-a-barrel-war-ship-squeeze-shortages)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (Dubai-linked Asian crude differentials)** — a deep OSP cut signals Saudi Arabia is chasing market share as exports normalise. Basis to Brent is the exposure, not flat price.

**Aramco's chief executive said the world's oil stockpiles have become "scarily thin", leaving markets exposed unless the Strait of Hormuz reopens.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-05/global-oil-supply-buffer-running-scarily-thin-aramco-ceo-says)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Brent volatility)** — thin inventories mean any new disruption hits price with little cushion. Long-dated options are cheap insurance against that.

**The G7 agreed to release 100 million barrels of oil, starting with diesel, and Donald Trump said he will hold off on a US diesel export ban.** — [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/energy/oil-gas/g7-nations-agree-to-release-100-million-barrels-of-oil-including-diesel-a-boost-for-trump/articleshow/134645922.cms) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-02/trump-says-us-to-hold-off-on-diesel-export-ban-after-g7-release)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (prompt ICE gasoil)** — released stocks land at the front of the curve. Expect the gasoil spread structure to soften before outright prices do.

**Yemeni government forces said they retook the Red Sea port of Mocha from the Houthis, moving closer to control of the Bab el-Mandeb strait that Saudi Arabia uses to export oil around Hormuz.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-05/yemen-says-it-s-retaken-mocha-as-battle-for-bab-el-mandeb-looms)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (Bab el-Mandeb chokepoint exposure)** — a fight for the strait could cut either way for Red Sea tanker traffic. War-risk premiums stay elevated until it is settled.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Web gap-fill from The Economic Times.*

**Eight Indian states, including Rajasthan, Gujarat and Maharashtra, plus Delhi are seeking extra central power allocations as weak hydro output and plant maintenance squeeze night-time supply.** — [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/energy/power/states-seek-additional-power-as-india-faces-night-time-supply-squeeze/articleshow/134721033.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Indian night-time exchange power)** — the central pool of 14–15 GW is already stretched. Non-solar hours carry the shortage risk.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Markets Daily.*

**About a third of the world's LNG exports came from the US in September, up from 25% last year, after a fifth of global supply was trapped behind Hormuz.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-10-05/the-world-risks-becoming-too-reliant-on-us-liquefied-natural-gas)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (US Gulf Coast concentration risk)** — one hurricane at the export plants now moves both TTF and JKM. Supply diversification has narrowed, not widened.

**LNG shipments through the Strait of Hormuz extended a rebound that began in September as producers try to ease the supply crunch.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-05/lng-trade-through-hormuz-extends-rebound-despite-shipping-risks)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (prompt JKM)** — returning Qatari cargoes chip at Asia's shortage. Attacks on ships keep the recovery fragile.

**Donald Trump threatened South Korea with retaliation if it does not invest in the Alaska LNG project, after Seoul said its participation depends on the project proving viable.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-02/trump-threatens-south-korea-over-alaska-lng-project-dispute)
