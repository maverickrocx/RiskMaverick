## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from The Economic Times' ET Markets Morning and Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Brent traded near $92.60 on Thursday after settling below $92, as reports said Donald Trump is weighing another round of extensive strikes on Iran.** — [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/commodities/news/oil-price-today-november-19-brent-crude-rises-to-93-as-trump-likely-weighs-fresh-iran-attacks-what-are-experts-saying/articleshow/135975416.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Brent implied volatility)** — a possible multi-day strike campaign is binary headline risk; upside optionality is a cleaner hedge than outright flat-price length.

**Crude is increasingly leaving the Strait of Hormuz on smaller tankers, as attacks on supertankers squeeze the availability of the ships that usually dominate the trade.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/smaller-tankers--hormuz-shuttle-trade-oil-flows-wti-brent-crude)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Gulf tanker freight)** — shuttling cargoes in smaller hulls lifts per-barrel freight and widens delivered spreads for Asian buyers.

**Alaska is forecast to add 23,000 barrels a day of output this year and about double that in 2027, per the EIA, as ConocoPhillips' Nuna and the first phase of Pikka ramp up.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-11-18/alaska-s-oil-renaissance-dawns-after-decades-of-decline)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (WTI 2027 strip)** — long-cycle barrels arrive regardless of price, adding supply the deferred curve cannot count on shale to withhold.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Britain's energy price cap is expected to rise 26% to about £2,166 a year in January, its highest since 2023, as the Iran war lifts wholesale gas and power prices.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/uk-energy-bills-seen-rising-26-biggest-hike-since-2022)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (Q1-27 NBP)** — the cap trails wholesale prices already traded; the live risk is political intervention if bills climb again in April.
