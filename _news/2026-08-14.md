## ⚡ Energy — oil

**Hormuz deal still elusive, and the tape is trading the headlines again.** Brent
traded near **$87/bbl** and WTI near **$81** on Friday, holding a decline after
Brent fell **2.2%** the previous session. Iran and Oman have yet to close a
reopening accord for the strait despite earlier-week optimism, and fresh attacks
on tankers and energy infrastructure have kept the market on edge.
*Risk lens:* six months into the crisis, price is still swinging on unconfirmed
deal headlines rather than confirmed flows — realised volatility keeps
outrunning any hedge sized on a "resolution is near" assumption.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-aug-14) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/10/oil-prices-stocks-surge-as-hormuz-closure-drags-on)

## 🔥 Gas & LNG

**European storage sits at an 18-year seasonal low, and TTF is climbing into it.**
Dutch front-month TTF rose **3.1%** to **€60.57/MWh** on 12 August as doubts grew
over a Hormuz accord; EU inventories were only **57.1%** full at the start of
August — the weakest for this time of year in almost two decades — after the
conflict choked off the LNG cargoes needed to restock.
*Risk lens:* a buyer short on physical LNG cargoes is exposed even if fully
hedged on the TTF curve — the shortfall is a volume problem the paper market
can't fix on its own, and it compounds every week storage stays behind norm.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/why-europe-s-gas-prices-and-storage-issues-are-causing-growing-concerns) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/eu-s-winter-gas-buffer-at-risk-as-storage-levels-hit-18-year-low)

**Henry Hub stays soft on record output.** The US benchmark slipped to
**$2.78/MMBtu** on 13 August, with EIA forecasting a **$2.87** average for
Q3 as Lower 48 production runs at a record pace and storage stays ample.
*Risk lens:* the US-Europe gas basis keeps widening — a domestic hedge does
nothing to cover a European or Asian LNG exposure right now.
— [EIA STEO](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/report/natgas.php)

## 🪙 Metals

**Copper sits near its record on tariff positioning, not demand.** The metal set
a fresh high near **$6.70/lb** on 5 August as traders front-ran a still-undecided
US tariff call — Commerce's June 30 deadline to recommend duties on refined
copper imports (phased from 15% in 2027 to 30% the year after) has passed
without an announcement, pulling record volumes of metal into US warehouses.
*Risk lens:* the US premium over LME is a policy-timing bet, not a fundamentals
signal — it can unwind fast the moment Washington actually rules.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/us-copper-trades-near-record-on-tariff-risks-hormuz-speculation) · [Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/copper-price-sets-fresh-us-record-as-tariff-driven-hoarding-meets-shrinking-supply/)

**Gold eases off a two-month high after an as-expected CPI print.** Bullion
touched **$4,449/oz** on 13 August, its best level since early June, then gave
back close to 1% as traders took profit once the inflation data landed in line
with forecasts.
*Risk lens:* a rally that stalls on an in-line, not a hot or cold, print says
positioning — not the macro case — is now the swing factor for near-term moves.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/08/13/gold-pauses-after-tame-us-inflation-fuels-rally-to-over-two-month-peak.html)

## 📊 Macro & cross-asset

**July CPI came in soft, and it moved the Fed odds more than the tape.**
Headline CPI rose **0.1%** on the month for a **3.4%** annual rate, down from
3.5% in June; core eased to **2.5%** from 2.6%. Treasury yields were little
changed on the print, with the 30-year at **5.254%**, but traders cut the odds
of a September hike to **42%** and now put a **64%** chance on a hold.
*Risk lens:* the energy-driven inflation impulse that kept hike risk alive
through July is cooling just as oil and gas volatility stays elevated — watch
whether the next CPI print (and the Hormuz outcome) reopens the hike debate
the Fed's July dissenters wanted.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/cpi-inflation-report-july-2026.html) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/treasury-yields-inflation-cpi-data.html)
