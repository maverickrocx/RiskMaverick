## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Evening Briefing Americas and Businessweek Daily, and The Economist's World in Brief.*

**Brent settled above $95 for the first time since late July and WTI closed above $90 as the US and Iran traded fresh strikes around the Strait of Hormuz; Trump said the renewed bombing "won't last too long."**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-sept-2) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/us-attacks-prompt-iranian-retaliation-as-fighting-intensifies)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (front-month Brent)** — the war premium rebuilds on each Hormuz exchange of fire, and Trump's "won't last long" framing is a headline risk both ways for the prompt contract.

**US pump gasoline is above $4 a gallon — the highest ever for the season — and diesel is nearing $6 as Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian refineries squeeze product supply; Treasury Secretary Bessent blamed those strikes, not the Iran war, for the price spike.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/us-diesel-hits-highest-since-april-as-wars-strain-global-supply) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/bessent-blames-high-energy-prices-on-ukraine-s-russia-strikes)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (distillate cracks)** — refinery outages, not crude, are setting the product price, so margins stay bid even if Brent softens on a ceasefire headline.

— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/kepco-seeks-billions-from-samsung-sk-hynix-to-finance-grid)
