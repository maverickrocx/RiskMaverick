date: 2026-08-14
sector: gas-power
standfirst: "Europe is heading into the heating season with the thinnest storage buffer in over a decade, and the reason is no longer infrastructure — it is that the forward curve does not pay anyone to fill it."
---

**Germany is going into winter with the emptiest gas stores since the energy
crisis.** Storage sites were about 41% full on 1 July, the lowest for that
date since 2021/22, and around 47% in early August — deep into an injection
season that should be well advanced. BloombergNEF puts the shortfall down to a
sluggish refill that slowed further in July, leaving the country needing its
strongest late-summer injections in years to reach the 76% booked level by
1 November.
— [Clean Energy Wire](https://www.cleanenergywire.org/news/germany-enters-heating-season-unexpectedly-low-storage-level-gas) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/why-europe-s-gas-prices-and-storage-issues-are-causing-growing-concerns)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (winter TTF)** — a low starting inventory converts any
cold snap or supply interruption from a price event into a physical one. The
summer/winter spread is the instrument that reprices first, and storage
operators who sold winter against unfilled capacity are carrying a short
physical position they may have to buy back into a rising market.

**The binding constraint is economic, not physical.** Germany's infrastructure
can still refill in time; what is missing is the price signal. Elevated prompt
prices driven by the Middle East war, plus higher gas burn for air
conditioning, have left the curve offering too little reward for injecting.
EU-wide inventories sat at about 57% of capacity in early August, the lowest
for that point in the year in records going back well over a decade.
— [European Gas Hub](https://europeangashub.com/weak-market-incentives-threaten-german-gas-storage-refill.html) · [Columbia CGEP](https://www.energypolicy.columbia.edu/european-gas-storage-the-worlds-winter-buffer-is-dwindling/)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (summer/winter spread as it stands)** — a backwardated
or flat curve that fails to fund storage economics is self-correcting, and it
usually corrects violently. Expect the spread to have to widen to clear the
market. For power, thin gas inventories raise the correlation between gas and
spark spreads exactly when hedge books assume they are independent.
