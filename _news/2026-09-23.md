## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Trump has confirmed he's pushing for a ban on US diesel exports — the world's largest source of the fuel, with Europe as the top importing region — a move analysts say would starve global markets without solving domestic price problems.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-09-23/a-us-diesel-export-ban-would-be-a-calamity-for-the-market)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (global diesel/crack spreads)** — removing the world's largest diesel exporter from global trade would starve importing regions like Europe just as refined-product markets are already tight.

**Saudi Arabia is running tests to resume its East-West oil pipeline as the US flagged progress in talks with Iran aimed at ending a war that has disrupted energy markets for almost seven months.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/saudi-arabia-running-tests-to-resume-east-west-oil-pipeline)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (Hormuz war-risk premium)** — a pipeline restart plus diplomatic progress both point toward easing, not tightening, near-term chokepoint-driven supply risk.
