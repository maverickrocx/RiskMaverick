## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily, Morning Briefing Americas and Markets Daily, and The Economic Times.*

**Goldman Sachs warned Brent could reach $120 a barrel if attacks on Middle East shipping intensify — and recommended natural gas and diesel positions to capture the upside — after the US and Iran traded their heaviest tanker strikes of the war and Brent settled near $97.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-07/goldman-flags-risk-of-120-oil-if-mideast-ship-attacks-intensify) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-06/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-september-7)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Brent call skew)** — a bank target a quarter above spot pulls demand up the call-strike ladder and steepens the upside volatility smile before any fresh attack lands.

**Middle East crude shipments have fallen to about 11 million barrels a day from 18 million before the war, with Hormuz flows briefly below 2 million and no large crude carrier seen leaving the strait since 2 September, yet analysts still put Brent's "fair" value near $95 on the running average.**
— [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/commodities/news/explainer-why-isnt-oil-above-100-despite-supply-disruptions/articleshow/133903924.cms) · [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/hormuz-traffic-slows-after-iran-threatens-retaliation-for-us-attacks/articleshow/133904705.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (Brent flat price)** — the market is pricing intermittent disruption, not closure, and the distance between spot and a genuine blockade is the size of the move still on the table.

**Iran said a deal with Oman to jointly manage shipping through the Strait of Hormuz is days away — briefly erasing oil's gains — even as it vowed further retaliation and called foreign energy firms' Gulf assets "sprawling, accessible and exposed"; the US is expected to oppose any accord.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-07/iran-says-strait-of-hormuz-deal-with-oman-just-days-away)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (front-month Brent, tail)** — a working Iran–Oman transit protocol would be the war's first concrete de-escalation mechanism and let some embedded closure premium bleed out of the prompt.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Markets Daily.*

**High energy costs helped carry Germany's far-right AfD to a record 44% in the Saxony-Anhalt state election: German gas import prices are roughly double their early-2021 level, pump prices are up almost 40% since Hormuz closed, and Dow plans to shut two energy-intensive chemical plants in the state by end-2027.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-09-07/high-energy-costs-helped-a-german-far-right-party-to-victory)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (German power & gas forwards, political tail)** — a far-right bloc campaigning to end Russia sanctions, reopen Nord Stream and extend coal past 2038 adds a low-probability, high-impact downside to the 2027-plus curve.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Morning Briefing Americas.*

**Qatar is sending empty LNG carriers back toward the Persian Gulf, a possible sign the world's second-largest supplier is preparing to resume exports through the Strait of Hormuz after weekend disruption.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-07/qatar-is-bringing-empty-lng-ships-home-in-possible-export-pivot)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (JKM–TTF spread)** — a Qatari restart would return roughly a fifth of global LNG supply to the water and undercut the scarcity premium Europe and Asia have bid into winter.
