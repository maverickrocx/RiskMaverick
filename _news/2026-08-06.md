## ⚡ Energy — oil

**Brent and WTI shed their war premium.** Brent slid 5.3% to **$79.36/bbl** and WTI
lost 5.7% to **$75.77/bbl** on Tuesday, holding those losses into Wednesday
(Brent **$79.26**, WTI **$75.69**), after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said a
deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz to commercial shipping could be struck "today
or tomorrow."
*Risk lens:* this is a premium unwind on headlines, not a fundamentals-driven
rebalancing — hedgers who added protection at the highs now carry the reversal
risk, and the move can snap back on any sign the talks stall.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-aug-5) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/oil-rises-after-selloff-as-talks-to-end-us-iran-war-remain-uncertain.html)

**The emerging Hormuz deal is a 60-day, revocable fix, not a permanent reopening.**
Under the framework reported this week, inbound ships would route through Iranian
territorial waters and outbound ships through Omani waters, with no transit tolls,
while the parties work to clear naval mines from the main shipping lane within 30
days — a permanent two-way arrangement would only follow that.
*Risk lens:* freight, war-risk insurance and voyage-time exposure stay live until
mines are actually cleared; a fragile 60-day arrangement is not a basis for
unwinding chokepoint hedges wholesale.
— [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/05/us-iran-strait-of-hormuz-deal-nears) · [investinglive](https://investinglive.com/commodities/us-iran-and-oman-near-interim-deal-to-reopen-strait-of-hormuz/)

## 🔥 Gas & LNG

**Henry Hub stays detached from the oil-driven volatility.** The prompt month has
drifted down near **$2.7/MMBtu** in early August — spot printed **$2.63/MMBtu** on
27 July — after a 30-session run above $3 ended on 10 July. Record Lower 48
production (EIA's 2026 outlook now sees output averaging **111.2 Bcf/d**) and
storage running roughly **6% above** the five-year average are keeping the
domestic balance loose through the summer.
*Risk lens:* US gas is trading almost entirely on its own supply/demand, not on
the Middle East risk premium moving oil, gold and rates together — a reminder
that a Henry Hub hedge does nothing for TTF or JKM exposure.
— [EIA](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/report/natgas.php) · [American Gas Association](https://www.aga.org/research-policy/resource-library/natural-gas-market-indicators-july-23-2026/)

## 🪙 Metals

**Copper closes in on $14,000/ton as tariff front-running peaks.** LME copper hit
**$14,000/ton**, a two-month high, as inflows to the US ran at their fastest pace
in at least 12 years of shipping data — more than **200,000 tons** landed at US
ports in July alone — ahead of the still-pending Trump administration decision on
Section 232 copper tariffs. Combined Comex and LME US inventories now exceed
**740,000 tons**.
*Risk lens:* the stockpile build is a one-off tariff-arbitrage flow, not organic
demand — once the tariff decision lands, the US import premium and the
LME–Comex spread are exposed to a sharp reversal.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/copper-marches-closer-to-14-000-as-flows-to-us-tighten-market) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/us-copper-inflows-surge-as-market-awaits-trump-tariff-call)

**Gold rallies on the same headline that is hitting oil.** Gold held above
**$4,100/oz**, pushing toward **$4,245** intraday Wednesday, as progress on the
Hormuz deal eased the energy-driven inflation pressure that had been building a
case for a Fed hike.
*Risk lens:* gold and oil moving in opposite directions off an identical catalyst
is a rates story, not a risk-on/risk-off split — useful context for anyone still
pricing gold purely as a war hedge.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/gold-holds-gain-as-progress-on-hormuz-deal-lowers-rate-hike-odds)

## 📊 Macro & cross-asset

**Equities hit fresh records on earnings plus Hormuz hopes, then pause.** The
S&P 500 jumped **1.79%** to a record **7,736.52** on Tuesday and the Dow added
**907 points (+1.71%)** to close above **54,000** for the first time, driven by
strong earnings (Palantir +29.5%) alongside the Hormuz optimism; both indexes then
paused Wednesday, with the S&P slipping **0.2%** and the Nasdaq down **0.8%**,
while the Dow eked out a fifth straight record close.
*Risk lens:* a rally stacked on two simultaneous catalysts — earnings-season
strength and geopolitical de-escalation — is vulnerable to either one
disappointing, and realized equity vol is being compressed by the same headlines
moving the commodity complex.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) · [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-aug-5-2026)

**Treasury yields fall in lockstep with oil.** The 10-year yield fell more than
**6bp to 4.619%**, the 2-year slipped **5bp to 4.198%** and the 30-year shed
**4bp to 5.182%** on Tuesday as Bessent's Hormuz comments hit crude — a
textbook read-through from lower energy-driven inflation risk to lower rate-hike
odds.
*Risk lens:* oil, gold and rates are now trading as one macro trade off a single
geopolitical headline stream — correlation across the book is unusually high
right now, cutting the diversification benefit precisely when it might be needed.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/treasury-yields-investors-peace-talks-uncertainty.html)
