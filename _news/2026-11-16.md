## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from The Economic Times' ET Markets Morning and Bloomberg's Morning Briefing Asia.*

**Brent slipped 0.5% to $95.37 on Monday after the IEA forecast fourth-quarter supply of 105 million barrels a day, 1.7 million above demand, as Hormuz shipments near pre-war levels.** — [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/commodities/news/oil-prices-fall-on-expectations-of-coming-supply-surplus/articleshow/135869595.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (Brent time spreads)** — a market repricing from deficit to surplus pressures backwardation; front-month length looks harder to defend.

**Iran said it launched six drones at an air base in Bahrain, its first such attack since early September, while insisting a path to ending the war with the US remains open.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-15/iran-strikes-bahrain-base-repeats-path-to-us-deal-remains-open)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (prompt Brent volatility)** — renewed strikes in the Gulf keep a geopolitical premium in options even as the surplus narrative weighs on outright prices.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Points of Return (John Authers).*

**Data-centre construction proposals went to a vote in 41 places at the US midterms, and all but one were rejected.** — [Bloomberg Opinion](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/newsletters/2026-11-16/after-the-huang-scandal-aindicators-look-shakier)
