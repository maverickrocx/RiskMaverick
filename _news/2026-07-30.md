## ⚡ Energy — oil

**Brent surges nearly 7% as US-Iran strikes resume.** Brent jumped about 6.8% on
Wednesday to **$89.79/bbl** and WTI rose 6.2% to **$84.20**, after renewed US and
Israeli strikes on Iran dashed hopes of an imminent de-escalation and Tehran repeated
its threat to control shipping through the Strait of Hormuz.
*Risk lens:* the war premium is round-tripping within days — hedgers who lifted
cover on ceasefire hopes are back exposed to the reversal, and realised volatility
on the front month is climbing again.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-july-29)

**Iran strikes Saudi energy assets; US intercepts a missile barrage.** The US
military said it shot down an Iranian missile barrage aimed at Gulf targets and
joined Saudi forces in striking Iran-backed militia sites in Iraq, while separate
reporting pointed to fresh attacks on Saudi energy infrastructure — pushing the
conflict's target set beyond the shipping lane that has dominated it since February.
*Risk lens:* onshore Gulf production and processing assets entering the target set
is a materially different exposure than a chokepoint blockade; offtakers of
Saudi-origin barrels should reassess force-majeure triggers now.
— [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/07/29/us-intercepts-iranian-attack-and-launches-joint-strikes-with-saudi-arabia-against-iran-bac) · [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/us-iran-war-trump-saudi-arabia-strait-of-hormuz/)

**Hormuz transits keep collapsing.** Weekly inbound tanker transits fell to roughly
25 in the week of 13–19 July, down from 108 the week before, and nearly 70% of
observed transits are now running "dark" with transponders off, up from 55% two
weeks earlier.
*Risk lens:* AIS-blind transits break the position data that freight and war-risk
insurance pricing depends on — premiums are being set against a shrinking
information set.
— [Lloyd's List Intelligence](https://www.lloydslistintelligence.com/resources/blog/strait-of-hormuz-brief-21-july-2026) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/17/iran-war-oil-tanker-strait-hormuz-traffic-attacks-trump.html)

## 🔥 Gas & LNG

**TTF snaps back on the same escalation.** The European benchmark rose about 2.3%
on Wednesday to trade near **€59/MWh**, clawing back most of an 8%+ drop earlier in
the week that had followed a brief lull in US-Iran hostilities.
*Risk lens:* the whipsaw shows how thin the geopolitical floor under TTF still is —
flat-price hedges are being repeatedly reversed by a conflict with no fixed
trajectory.
— [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/eu-natural-gas)

**Henry Hub stays anchored near $2.90.** The US benchmark has held a tight range
through late July on record production and storage running above the five-year
average, decoupling further from the European move.
*Risk lens:* the Atlantic basis is about as wide as it gets right now — a
domestic gas book offers little protection against the European or Asian LNG leg
of an exposure.
— [EIA STEO](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/)

## 🔌 Power & carbon

**EUAs hold the ETS-reform bid.** Carbon allowances remain above **€80/t**, having
spiked toward €86 after the European Commission's 17 July proposal to slow the
post-2030 emissions-cap reduction rate and fund a €100bn Industrial
Decarbonisation Bank — even as the Middle East energy shock competes for market
attention.
*Risk lens:* policy, not the energy complex, is still setting the marginal price
of carbon; compliance buyers rolling positions into year-end should treat the
reform timeline, not oil, as the key event risk.
— [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/news/eu-commission-publishes-eu-ets-review-proposal) · [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/carbon)

## 🪙 Metals

**Gold pops on the hawkish hold.** Spot gold slipped below $4,000/oz intraday
Wednesday — a level last seen in mid-July — then rebounded about 1.3% to close
near **$4,081/oz**, as the Fed's rate hold and the fresh Mideast escalation
revived haven demand.
*Risk lens:* a hold that still reads as risk-negative for equities is exactly the
environment where gold decouples from real yields — correlation-based hedges
built on the old relationship will misfire.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/gold-ticks-higher-as-markets-brace-for-fed-decision.html) · [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/gold)

**Copper eased to around $6.27/lb** on Wednesday but remains up roughly 36% over
the past year, still underpinned by tight scrap availability and structural
demand from grid buildout and AI data centres.
— [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/copper) · [S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/2026/04/copper-gold-market-outlook-2026-prices-supply-mining-costs)

## 📊 Macro & cross-asset

**The Fed holds — but for the first time since 2016, three officials dissent the
same way.** The FOMC kept the funds rate at **3.50–3.75%** on a 9–3 vote, with
Cleveland's Beth Hammack, Minneapolis's Neel Kashkari and Dallas's Lorie Logan all
pushing for a quarter-point hike on inflation that has stayed above target.
*Risk lens:* a unified hawkish dissent of this size is a signal, not noise — it
raises the odds that the easing path priced into curves and carry trades gets
pushed further out.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/fed-holds-rates-steady-three-officials-dissent-favoring-a-hike) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/fed-meeting-today-live-updates.html)

**Equities post their worst session since April 2025.** The Dow fell 2.19%
(-1,153 points) to **51,594**, the S&P 500 dropped 1.52% to **7,316** and the
Nasdaq Composite lost 1.74%, pushing the Nasdaq 100 into correction territory as
the 10-year Treasury yield jumped 7bp to above **4.67%** and the 30-year rose 10bp
above 5.2%.
*Risk lens:* rates, oil and equity vol all moved the same direction in one
session — a reminder that cross-asset correlation breaks down exactly when
diversified books need it most.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/fed-meeting-today-live-updates.html)
