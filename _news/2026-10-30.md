## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**Oil eased as traders weighed fresh diplomatic efforts to avert a resumption of US strikes on Iran, a prospect that has pushed prices higher in recent weeks.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-28/oil-declines-as-traders-parse-signs-of-fresh-diplomacy-on-iran)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (front-month Brent)** — the geopolitical premium is being traded headline to headline, so hedges should stay in place rather than chase the dip.

**US Gulf Coast refining margins have more than tripled this year to an all-time high of $72 a barrel in September, with Europe and Asia posting similar gains.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-28/global-conflicts-spur-historic-comeback-for-oil-refining-profits) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-29/shell-reports-second-highest-profit-ever-as-refining-hits-record)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (gasoline and diesel cracks)** — record margins signal product tightness that keeps crack spreads supported against crude.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**QatarEnergy agreed to buy US LNG from Woodside Energy over two decades and says it is now the top buyer of American LNG as it diversifies away from the Middle East.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-29/qatar-agrees-to-buy-lng-from-woodside-in-second-long-term-deal)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (long-dated LNG contracts)** — more term volume tied to US supply gradually reduces Atlantic-basin spot exposure rather than moving prompt prices.
