## ⚡ Energy — oil

**Brent surges back above $87 as the Hormuz deal stays out of reach.** Brent settled near **$87.69/bbl** on 10 August, up almost **5%** on the day, while WTI rose to **$80.42** (+2.86%) after touching an intraday high of **$81.44** (+4.2%). The move came as Iran and Oman remained short of a deal to restore normal shipping through the Strait of Hormuz and Houthi forces claimed a fresh attack on Saudi energy infrastructure.
*Risk lens:* the third headline-driven repricing in as many weeks — hedgers who lifted cover on the last de-escalation are now carrying the reversal again.
— [Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/price-of-oil-08-10-2026/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-aug-10)

**A refinery and a tanker were hit inside the same week.** A Houthi drone struck Saudi Aramco's refinery in Jizan on 9 August, starting a fire that was later extinguished with no casualties reported; days earlier, on 5 August, Houthi forces said they hit the Saudi-flagged tanker *Wafa* with ballistic missiles off Yanbu in the Red Sea. Iran and Oman have separately agreed on shipping-route coordinates for the Strait, but Iran's foreign minister says there are no active talks with Washington and Tehran won't reopen Hormuz "until America corrects its behaviour."
*Risk lens:* two live threats are now stacked — a possible Hormuz opening and a widening Red Sea front — so freight, war-risk insurance and voyage-time exposure are being priced for both outcomes at once, not one.
— [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/saudi-arabia-says-fire-extinguished-at-aramco-facility-in-jizan) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/iran-oman-us-close-to-hormuz-deal-what-do-they-all-want)

## 🔥 Gas & LNG

**Henry Hub climbs on record LNG feedgas demand.** US gas futures rose about **4%** on 10 August to a one-week high near **$2.757/MMBtu**, as daily flows to LNG export terminals climbed toward a one-month high of **17.9 bcf/d** even with Lower 48 production running at a record pace this month.
*Risk lens:* export-terminal utilisation, not domestic supply, is now the marginal driver of the US hub — that tightens the correlation between Henry Hub and international gas benchmarks just as basis trades assume it stays loose.
— [EnergyNow](https://energynow.com/2026/08/us-natgas-prices-climb-4-as-lng-export-flows-hit-one-month-high-2/)

**TTF eases even as European storage lags the seasonal norm.** European gas has slipped toward **€53–54/MWh**, down from **€59.44** at end-July, but EU storage sits around **55%** of capacity versus a five-year average near **71%** — roughly 15 bcm short — while LNG imports are running about **23%** below their usual pace.
*Risk lens:* a falling front-month price against a storage deficit heading into autumn is a market betting the chokepoint risk fades before the weather turns, not a resolved balance.
— [Energy Aspects](https://www.energyaspects.com/resources/insights/europe-gas-storage-ttf-outlook-summer-2026) · [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/eu-natural-gas/news/514334)

## 🪙 Metals

**Copper pulls back from a record high as tariff hoarding meets profit-taking.** Copper touched an all-time high near **$6.90/lb** on 6 August before slipping below **$6.60/lb** on 10 August as traders locked in gains; the LME market has flipped into backwardation, with cash copper commanding a premium of more than **$100** over three-month metal — the widest since January. Washington's decision on phased tariffs on refined copper imports remains pending, and shipments into the US are still running at record volumes.
*Risk lens:* a market simultaneously hoarding against a tariff that hasn't been announced and unwinding length on the same headline is a volatility, not a direction, story — basis between US and international copper is the exposure to watch.
— [Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/copper-price-pulls-back-from-record-territory-as-war-jitters-trump-earnings-beats/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/us-copper-trades-near-record-on-tariff-risks-hormuz-speculation)

**Gold sets a fresh record on the back of the weak jobs print.** Gold rose to **$4,378.76/oz** on 10 August, up roughly **31%** year-on-year, extending a rally that began with Friday's soft July payrolls data and firming bets on Fed rate cuts.
*Risk lens:* gold's move is now tracking the labour-market and rate-path story more than the geopolitical premium sitting in oil — the two safe-haven trades are no longer moving in lockstep.
— [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-prices-today-friday-august-7-2026-gold-prices-continue-to-rise-even-after-july-jobs-report-misses-122102132.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/current-price-of-gold-08-10-2026/)

## 📊 Macro & cross-asset

**July payrolls shrank instead of growing, and the miss was large.** US nonfarm payrolls fell by **23,000** in July against consensus for an **83,000** gain, while June's already-soft print was revised to just **+20,000**. Job losses were concentrated in local-government education (−50,000) and retail (−19,000); private payrolls still rose 30,000. The unemployment rate eased to **4.1%** from 4.2%, and annual wage growth slowed to **3.2%**, its lowest since May 2021.
*Risk lens:* a payrolls contraction with a falling unemployment rate is an unusual mix — it reads as a shrinking labour force as much as outright weakness, which is a harder signal for the Fed to act on cleanly.
— [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/07/jobs-report-july-2026.html)

**Rate-hike odds fell sharply, and equities wobbled on oil instead.** Fed funds-futures pricing for a September hike dropped from roughly 57% to 44% within hours of the payrolls report. Equities largely shrugged off the rates move: the S&P 500 slipped about 0.1% to close near **7,753** on 10 August, with Nasdaq and Dow also lower, as the day's 5% jump in oil prices — not the jobs data — drove the session.
*Risk lens:* when a soft labour print fails to rally risk assets because an oil spike is the bigger line item that day, it's a reminder that energy risk is currently overriding the rates channel in cross-asset books.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/stock-market-today-dow-s-p-live-updates) · [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-aug-10-2026)
