## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**New US legislation lets Trump impose tariffs of up to 100% on major buyers of Russian oil, including India, which currently sources roughly 40% of its crude from Moscow.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-09-18/donald-trump-just-made-india-s-oil-balancing-act-even-trickier)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (non-Russian crude premium)** — forcing India to replace up to 40% of its Russian-linked barrels would tighten the pool of alternative supply available to the market.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**At least two LNG shipments transited the Strait of Hormuz this week as producers push for more transits through a market still short of supply and facing surging prices.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-18/more-lng-getting-through-hormuz-as-producers-push-for-transits)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (JKM/TTF basis)** — a modest pickup in transits doesn't retire the chokepoint premium still priced into global LNG.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Green Daily.*

**The UK's energy watchdog proposed new fees on a ballooning queue of battery-storage projects awaiting grid connections, aiming to squeeze out those unlikely to ever be built.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-17/uk-seeks-to-slap-fees-on-battery-projects-clogging-up-grid-queue)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (UK grid-connection queue)** — clearing speculative applications should speed real storage capacity online, but the near-term effect is fewer, not faster, connections.
