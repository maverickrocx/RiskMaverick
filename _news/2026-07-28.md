## ⚡ Energy — oil

**Brent unwinds the war premium.** The global benchmark lost roughly 8.7% on Monday — its steepest one-day fall in months — sliding toward **$87/bbl** after President Trump said the US and Iran were negotiating an end to hostilities and both sides held off further strikes; Brent eased further to **$86.58** on Tuesday.
*Risk lens:* a pure geopolitical-premium move, not a balance move — hedgers who added protection into the spike now carry the reversal risk.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-july-28) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/price-of-oil-07-27-2026/)

**Gulf flows still well short of normal.** The IEA's July report puts total Gulf oil exports, including volumes bypassing the Strait, at **16.1 mb/d** in June — up 6.5 mb/d on the ceasefire but still far below the roughly **24 mb/d** pre-war average — and flags the 7–8 July escalation as a fresh cloud over that recovery. Separately, Kazakhstan's CPC terminal, which handles more than 80% of the country's oil exports, resumed loading on 27 July after a week-long shutdown caused by drone strikes on four tankers.
*Risk lens:* chokepoint and terminal risk hasn't gone away just because flat price has fallen — freight, war-risk cover and voyage timing remain live exposures.
— [IEA](https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2026) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/kazakh-oil-port-cpc-set-to-load-tankers-again-after-drone-pause)

## 🔥 Gas & LNG

**Henry Hub slips below $2.80.** US gas futures fell more than 3.5% to **$2.77/MMBtu** on Monday, the lowest since 8 May, as the Middle East de-escalation removed a geopolitical bid while EIA data show inventories running **6.4%** above the five-year seasonal average and Lower-48 output near **110.4 bcfd**.
— [EIA](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/report/natgas.php) · [American Gas Association](https://www.aga.org/research-policy/resource-library/natural-gas-market-indicators-july-23-2026/)

**TTF gives back its Hormuz spike.** European gas had jumped as far as the high-€50s/MWh earlier in July on fears of a second LNG-supply disruption; it fell back sharply on Monday's ceasefire news, tracking crude lower.
*Risk lens:* textbook basis behaviour — TTF priced the disruption risk while Henry Hub barely moved. A US-hub hedge would not have covered a European exposure.
— [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/eu-natural-gas) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/21/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-houthis.html)

## 🔌 Power & carbon

**EUAs hold above €80 into the Phase-5 reform.** December-2026 EU carbon allowances traded between **€79.4 and €82/t** through July, with speculative long positions building ahead of the European Commission's 17 July proposal to reform the ETS for 2031–2040, including a slower allowance-reduction path and a new industrial-decarbonisation fund.
*Risk lens:* policy, not power demand, remains the dominant driver of carbon price into year-end.
— [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/carbon) · [S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/natural-gas/061026-european-commission-lays-out-core-priorities-for-ets-july-review)

## 🪙 Metals

**Copper holds near $6.30/lb.** The metal was little changed at **$6.29/lb** — LME three-month down 0.5% at **$13,664/t** — on Tuesday, up around 3% on the month and roughly 11.5% year-on-year, as AI-data-centre and clean-energy demand offset caution ahead of the Fed decision.
— [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/copper) · [CME Group](https://www.cmegroup.com/newsletters/micro-gold-silver-and-copper-monthly-update/2026-07-micro-metals-products-update.html)

**Gold sits in "no-man's land."** Bullion is trading between its 200-day average near **$4,340/oz** and 50-day average near **$4,730/oz**, with J.P. Morgan Research still targeting **$6,000/oz** by year-end even as Fed hike risk keeps the metal on the back burner for now.
— [J.P. Morgan](https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/global-research/commodities/gold-prices)

## 📊 Macro & cross-asset

**The Fed decides Wednesday, and a hike is back in play.** The FOMC is widely expected to hold its funds rate at **3.50–3.75%** for a fifth straight meeting under new chair Kevin Warsh, but rate-hike odds have jumped to roughly **38%** — up from about 16% a week earlier — as the oil-and-gas spike earlier in July revived inflation risk.
*Risk lens:* an energy-driven inflation impulse is the hardest kind for a central bank to look through, and it links commodity risk straight into rates and FX books.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/fed-interest-rates-july.html) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/13/-a-july-rate-hike-from-the-fed-the-odds-are-rising.html)

**A chip-stock rout drags equities.** Tuesday's premarket session saw Asian memory makers slammed — SK Hynix down as much as 13%, Samsung down up to 10%, dragging Korea's Kospi down about 10% — on fears that circular AI-financing arrangements could unravel if hyperscaler capex slows. US futures followed lower even as Monday's oil pullback eased the 10-year Treasury yield to around **4.64%**.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/daily-open-ai-boom-chip-selloff-sk-hynix.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/stock-market-today-dow-s-p-live-updates)
