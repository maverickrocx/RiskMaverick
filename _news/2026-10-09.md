## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily and Markets Daily.*

**Oil gained after a report that the White House asked the Pentagon to draw up Iran strike options that could be executed before the midterm elections, with Brent at $104.40, up 4.2%, in early trading.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-07/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-date)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (front-month Brent)** — headline-driven geopolitical premium returns. Expect sharp gaps in implied volatility.

**UK Maritime Trade Operations reported ten attacks in and around Hormuz in the first week of October against 16 in all of September, yet crude shipments are near prewar levels.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-08/supertankers-racing-to-middle-east-worsen-global-ships-crunch) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-10-08/resilient-middle-east-oil-trade-learns-to-live-with-war)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (Hormuz chokepoint exposure)** — flows are holding, but the risk has moved to freight and insurance rather than barrels.

**Saudi Arabia is in talks with customers to offer long-term oil loadings outside the Strait of Hormuz next year, formalizing a system it has used during the war.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-08/saudis-in-talks-to-formalize-hormuz-shuttles-in-fight-for-market)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (Middle East crude differentials)** — a market-share fight points to tighter premiums and more pricing competition.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Energy Daily.*

**At least 12 LNG carriers owned or chartered by QatarEnergy, roughly 2% of global LNG fleet capacity, have gathered in the Gulf in a potential export boost.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-07/qatar-brings-empty-lng-ships-into-gulf-in-potential-export-boost)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (Asian spot LNG)** — a Qatari restart would ease supply, but the cargoes still need to transit Hormuz.
