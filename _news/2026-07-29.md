## ⚡ Energy — oil

**Iran's intercepted strike whipsaws the barrel.** Brent settled 4.8% lower at
**$84.09/bbl** on Tuesday as Iran, Saudi Arabia and Oman discussed reopening the
Strait of Hormuz — then, hours later, Iranian forces fired multiple ballistic
missiles at US bases in the Middle East in what Washington called an "attempted
surprise attack." US Central Command said every missile was intercepted, and WTI
jumped 4.4% to **$82.73/bbl** on the news.
*Risk lens:* a near-5% intraday reversal on a single geopolitical headline is a
reminder that any hedge lifted into the truce rally is now working against the book.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/oil-price-today-wti-brent-us-iran-hormuz.html)

**Hormuz throughput remains impaired.** Even during the recent lull, tanker
transits through the strait have averaged around **13 vessels a day**, versus a
pre-war norm above 30, as owners route cargoes elsewhere or hold them back.
*Risk lens:* freight and war-risk insurance costs are being driven by a
structurally impaired chokepoint, not just today's headlines — that exposure
doesn't reprice down with a one-day ceasefire.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/iran-us-skirmishes-worsen-as-hormuz-shipping-traffic-dwindles)

## 🔥 Gas & LNG

**Henry Hub sinks on record output.** US producers pushed Lower 48 output to
around **110.6 bcfd** in July, matching December's record, while storage stood
**6.4% above** the five-year average after a 32 Bcf injection in the week to 17
July — an oversupplied domestic balance that has pulled the prompt month toward
its lowest level in months.
*Risk lens:* Henry Hub is decoupled from the geopolitical premium sitting in oil —
basis risk between the US benchmark and internationally exposed gas books stays
wide.
— [EIA](https://www.eia.gov/naturalgas/weekly/) · [EIA STEO](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/report/natgas.php)

## 🪙 Metals

**Chile's storms keep copper's supply story alive.** A severe winter storm system
is sweeping Chile's copper belt, the world's largest, threatening output at some
of the country's biggest mines just as a China-driven tightness narrative was
already underpinning prices.
*Risk lens:* with roughly a quarter of global mined supply concentrated in one
weather-exposed corridor, copper's tight balance carries real physical tail risk,
not just a demand story.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/giant-copper-mines-brace-for-winter-deluge-in-top-producer-chile) · [S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/2026/04/copper-gold-market-outlook-2026-prices-supply-mining-costs)

**Gold eases into the Fed decision.** Spot gold fell about 1.1% to roughly
**$4,020/oz** on Tuesday as the dollar held near a one-month high, with
interest-rate swaps pricing an unusually high one-in-three chance of a Fed hike
this week.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/gold-edges-lower-as-traders-assess-prospects-for-fed-rate-hike)

## 📊 Macro & cross-asset

**The Fed decides today**, with the funds rate at **3.50–3.75%**. A hold is the
consensus call, but swaps pricing a roughly one-in-three chance of a hike this
close to the decision is unusual by recent standards.
*Risk lens:* energy-driven inflation is the hardest impulse for a central bank to
look through, and it's what's putting a live hike back on the table — that links
commodity risk straight into rates and FX books.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/gold-edges-lower-as-traders-assess-prospects-for-fed-rate-hike)

**A chip-stock rout is deepening risk-off in Asia.** Samsung Electronics closed
**13.4% lower** — its worst one-day fall in almost two decades — and SK Hynix
dropped **14.7%** after reports that a Chinese state-backed firm has begun mass
production of DUV lithography equipment, reviving competition fears on top of
existing worries about AI infrastructure spending. A Bloomberg gauge of Asian
semiconductor shares slumped **7.5%**, and Nasdaq 100 futures fell about 1%.
*Risk lens:* a single-sector, single-region shock spilling into broad US tech
futures is a reminder that equity-linked commodity demand stories — AI-driven
power and copper demand — sit on top of a crowded, volatile trade.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/korean-stocks-sink-as-chipmakers-plung-on-deepening-ai-fatigue)
