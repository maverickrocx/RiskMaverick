## Oil / Products {#oil-products}

*Sourced from The Economic Times' Morning Newsletter, Bloomberg's Morning Briefing Asia and Bloomberg Opinion.*

**Brent rose $1.32 to $105.64 a barrel early Monday after Donald Trump rejected Iran's plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and Tehran said it would not soften its seven-day proposal.** — [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/commodities/news/oil-heads-higher-as-us-iran-peace-talks-in-stalemate/articleshow/134529749.cms) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-27/iran-says-won-t-soften-demands-after-trump-rejects-hormuz-offer)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (front-month Brent volatility)** — with talks stalled but Trump expecting them to resume this week, prices are hostage to headlines. Expect overnight gaps in both directions.

**WTI lost 7.9% last week while Brent edged up 0.4%, on concern that Washington could ban diesel exports to cap record US diesel prices.** — [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/commodities/news/oil-heads-higher-as-us-iran-peace-talks-in-stalemate/articleshow/134529749.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (Brent–WTI spread)** — an export curb would strand US barrels at home and tighten Europe. Books hedging Brent-linked exposure with WTI carry widening basis risk.

**Japan, which imports 99.9% of its oil, is signalling a move away from Middle Eastern supply after the Hormuz shock, Javier Blas argues.** — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/articles/2026-09-28/can-japan-say-goodbye-to-middle-eastern-oil)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (Dubai-linked crude)** — a slow reallocation, not a flow shock. It matters for term supply contracts more than for prompt spreads.

## Gas & Power {#gas-power}

*Sourced from The Economic Times.*

**NYMEX Henry Hub has traded around $2.50–$3.50/MMBtu through the West Asia crisis, held down by US dry-gas output that EIA estimates will reach about 110.6 Bcf/d in 2026.** — [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/commodities/news/natural-gas-defies-energy-shock-as-strong-supply-keeps-prices-in-check/articleshow/134515059.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Bearish (prompt Henry Hub)** — domestic supply, not geopolitics, sets the US price. Oil-gas cross hedges have lost effectiveness this year.

## LNG {#lng}

*Sourced from The Economic Times.*

**Gulf LNG loadings fell about 35 bcm year on year between March and June, according to the IEA, while non-Gulf output rose nearly 18%, or around 27 bcm, softening the supply shock.** — [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/commodities/news/natural-gas-defies-energy-shock-as-strong-supply-keeps-prices-in-check/articleshow/134515059.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Bullish (winter JKM/TTF volatility)** — the cushion rests on Asian demand destruction and mild weather. A cold snap with Qatar still constrained leaves little spare supply.

## Carbon {#carbon}

*Sourced from The Economic Times.*

**An ICRIER working paper estimates India's steel exports to the EU could fall 24% under the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism, and Britain has agreed to recognise India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme for its own levy.** — [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/foreign-trade/india-exports-carbon-market-ccts-cbam-europe-new-border-tax/articleshow/134531752.cms)

*Risk lens:* **Neutral (EUA)** — Indian mills selling into Europe now carry EU carbon price exposure through CBAM. Few have ever hedged it.
