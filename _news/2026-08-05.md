## ⚡ Energy — oil

**Oil crashed on Hormuz deal hopes, then a Houthi tanker claim tested the rally's edges.**
Brent's October contract fell **5.3%** on Tuesday to settle at **$79.36/bbl** — the
lowest since 10 July — and WTI's September contract dropped **5.7%** to **$75.77**, as
the US, Iran and Oman signalled progress on an interim arrangement to manage shipping
through the Strait of Hormuz (inbound vessels via Iranian waters, outbound via Oman's).
Wednesday, prices ground lower still — Brent down **0.74%** to **$78.77**, WTI down
**1%** to **$74.93** — even after Yemen's Houthis claimed a missile strike on a Saudi
tanker near the export port of Yanbu and said they would target tankers further into
the northern Red Sea.
*Risk lens:* the market shrugging off a claimed tanker strike to keep trading the
Hormuz-deal headline is itself a signal — volatility is being sold into a
de-escalation story even though the underlying dispute (the US blockade linkage, who
controls the strait) isn't settled, and a second chokepoint risk is reopening in
parallel.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/oil-prices-iran-war-houthis-saudi-tanker.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/yemen-s-houthis-say-they-ll-target-tankers-in-northern-red-sea)

## 🔥 Gas & LNG

**TTF gave back part of its war premium alongside oil.** The European benchmark, at
**€59.44/MWh** on 31 July (+30% on end-June), fell about **2.2%** on Tuesday to
roughly **€56.50/MWh** after Trump said direct US-Iran talks were scheduled and that
he'd called off a planned strike to let negotiations continue — easing fears over the
roughly one-fifth of global LNG supply that transits Hormuz.
*Risk lens:* the same headline-driven regime as oil — a benchmark up 30% on the month
can give back a chunk of that move in a single session on a diplomatic signal alone.
Static hedges sized on July's vol surface are exposed on both sides of this trade.
— [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/eu-natural-gas/news/541615)

**Henry Hub stayed decoupled from the war premium.** The US benchmark traded near
**$2.69/MMBtu** on 5 August, little changed on the day, even as LNG feedgas deliveries
held near record levels above **17 Bcf/d**.
*Risk lens:* the basis between Henry Hub and export-linked demand is the thing to
watch, not the domestic print — a further outage at an export facility would show up
first in that spread.
— [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/natural-gas) · [EIA](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/report/natgas.php)

## 🔌 Power & carbon

**Europe's heatwave keeps curtailing its cleanest power sources.** France's
river-cooled nuclear fleet has logged a record amount of heat-related output cuts this
summer, Hungary's only nuclear station faces shutdown risk as river levels fall, and
Alpine and Nordic hydro output has been hobbled by the same hot, dry conditions —
forcing the continent to lean more on imported fossil fuels and variable renewables
just as demand peaks.
*Risk lens:* unlike the Hormuz story, this is a slow-building, weather-linked supply
constraint rather than a headline event — it argues for structural summer power-vol
and clean spark spread hedges rather than event-driven options.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-01/europe-s-endless-heat-waves-imperil-once-reliable-power-sources)

## 🪙 Metals

**Gold and silver rallied on the same news that's crushing oil.** Gold climbed toward
**$4,200/oz** on Wednesday — a third straight session of gains, touching **$4,245.80**
in early trading — and silver crested **$62/oz** for the first time since 6 July,
reaching **$62.27**, as investors weighed reports of an imminent Hormuz deal that could
ease inflation risk and reduce the odds of a near-term Fed hike.
*Risk lens:* gold rallying alongside equities while oil collapses is not a classic
risk-off bid — it's a rates trade on receding hike odds. A book still hedged with gold
as a pure geopolitical hedge is exposed if that correlation flips once the Hormuz
outcome is actually known.
— [Yahoo Finance (gold)](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-prices-today-wednesday-august-5-2026-gold-prices-are-holding-awaiting-news-on-hormuz-deal-120800834.html) · [Yahoo Finance (silver)](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/silver-prices-today-wednesday-august-5-2026-prices-rising-in-early-trading-on-hormuz-optimism-122145083.html)

**Copper hit a two-month high on critically thin LME stocks.** Benchmark three-month
copper rose as much as **1.3%** to **$14,053/tonne** on Tuesday — its highest since 2
June — after warehouse data showed a further 7,225-tonne withdrawal, leaving available
LME inventory at just **94,200 tonnes**, little more than a day of global consumption.
The cash-to-three-month spread widened to a **$105/tonne** backwardation, the steepest
since January.
*Risk lens:* backwardation this steep is a physical-tightness signal, not a
directional call — it raises rolling costs for any short hedge and increases the
chance of a delivery squeeze if withdrawals continue.
— [LME](https://www.lme.com/metals/non-ferrous/lme-copper) · [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/copper)

## 📊 Macro & cross-asset

**Equities hit records on AI earnings and the same Mideast de-escalation.** The S&P
500 rose **1.79%** to **7,736.52** on Tuesday — its first record close in two months —
and the Dow added **907.47 points (+1.71%)** to **54,085.88**, led by Palantir
(**+29.5%**, its biggest one-day gain since February 2024) and Caterpillar (**+5.6%**)
after both raised revenue forecasts. The 10-year Treasury yield fell to **4.619%** the
same day, as falling oil reduced near-term inflation pressure.
*Risk lens:* risk assets and the energy complex are trading the identical headline in
opposite directions — a portfolio still running an implicit "oil up, equities down"
geopolitical hedge is now short a correlation that just inverted.
— [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) · [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/sp-500-dow-hit-new-highs-ai-earnings-iran-rcna590834)
