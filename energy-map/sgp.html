Singapore

A city-state that makes almost all its power from imported gas. In 2026 Middle East fuel prices reached Singapore's tariffs with a one-quarter lag, and Indonesia began trimming pipeline gas.

SUMMARY

At a glance

  • Tariffs follow fuel with a lag. The Q4 2026 household tariff is 28.59 ¢/kWh before GST, down 10.4% on Q3 as fuel costs eased (SP Group).
  • 95% gas. Imported natural gas generates about 95% of Singapore's electricity (EMA).
  • Indonesian supply is shrinking. Indonesia began trimming pipeline exports by about 31 million cubic feet a day; EMA says that is under 2% of daily gas demand (Bloomberg).
  • LNG fills the gap, so Singapore buys more spot LNG in a tight market.
  • Carbon tax at S$45/t for 2026–27.
The call

Singapore is a pure LNG-price market with a quarterly lag. Spot LNG costs in one quarter set household tariffs in the next.
