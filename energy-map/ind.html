India

The world's fastest-growing large power market, run on coal with a fast-rising solar fleet. In 2026 demand hit records while the Iran war cut LNG to gas plants, and the government moved to stockpile coal.

SUMMARY

At a glance

  • Record demand. Peak demand reached 270.8 GW in May 2026 and topped 270 GW twice more in July (Ministry of Power).
  • Demand up 12% at peak in April–June on a year earlier, linked to heat.
  • Coal carries the load: about 69.5% of supply in April–June.
  • Gas power hit a six-year low as the Iran war cut LNG shipments (Bloomberg).
  • Coal stockpiles are being tripled before next summer after a supply crunch caused blackouts (Bloomberg).
The call

Indian power is a coal-logistics trade with an LNG option. Gas plants run only when LNG is affordable; in 2026 it was not, so coal stocks and rail capacity decide whether summer peaks are met.

04

Prices

  • Exchange prices rise with heat and fall when solar output is strong.
  • Retail tariffs are regulated, so price stress lands on utility finances and on shortages.
