Netherlands

Home of TTF, the benchmark that prices European gas, in a country that now imports almost all it burns. In 2026 the Dutch entered winter with storage cut to a deliberately lower target.

SUMMARY

At a glance

  • TTF is the world's gas price this year. It more than tripled during 2026 as Qatari LNG went offline (Bloomberg), and every Dutch power and gas price follows it.
  • Power is about 40% dearer than in 2025 so far this year, with gas the largest source of Dutch generation.
  • Storage is low by choice. The government cut the filling target from 115 TWh to 93 TWh at the European Commission's request; storage was 62.2% full on 11 November against a five-year average of 81.9% (Gasunie, GIE AGSI+).
  • LNG is the entry point. Two terminals, Gate in Rotterdam and EemsEnergyTerminal in Eemshaven, replace the closed Groningen field.
  • A net power exporter, mostly to Belgium.
The call

A Dutch position is a TTF position. With less gas in store than usual, a cold late winter would have to be met by LNG bought at the margin, and TTF would carry that cost to every market priced off it.

  • Gas is the largest source, followed by solar and wind, which together now produce more than gas.
  • Coal is still running. Coal-fired output rose from about 11 TWh to 14 TWh in the year to mid-November as gas got more expensive (Energy-Charts).
  • Exports to Belgium grew as Belgian nuclear went offline for much of the year.
  • Gas demand gets met by imports. Domestic production is a fraction of what it was; pipelines from Norway and LNG fill the gap.
04

Prices

  • Gas sets the price in most hours, so Dutch power tracks TTF plus carbon.
  • The year turned in summer. Monthly averages rose from about €125/MWh in August to €152 in September and €172 in October; the first part of November averaged about €268/MWh.
  • Fewer negative prices: 409 hours so far, against 581 in the same period of 2025.
  • TTF itself averaged about €87/MWh in October against €32 a year earlier.

Source: Energy-Charts, EPEX SPOT day-ahead NL; TTF from ICE Endex front month. Our averages.
