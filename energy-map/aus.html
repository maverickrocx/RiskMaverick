Australia

A major LNG exporter whose east-coast power market is being remade by rooftop solar, utility solar and batteries. In 2026 Australian power got cheaper while its LNG exporters sold into the tightest Asian market since 2022.

SUMMARY

At a glance

  • Power prices fell. The NEM averaged $63/MWh in the September quarter, down 27% on a year earlier and the lowest September quarter since 2021 (AEMO).
  • Batteries now set the price in almost half of dispatch intervals, up from 18% a year earlier (AEMO).
  • Record renewables. 44.3% of NEM generation in Q3, a September-quarter record; gas-fired output was the lowest for a third quarter since 2000.
  • LNG exports at a record. Queensland LNG export demand rose 16 PJ to its highest Q3 level on record.
  • Gas reservation is coming. From 1 July 2027 exporters must offer up to 20% of exports to the domestic market (Department of Industry).
The call

Australia is two markets: a power market where solar and batteries are pushing prices down, and an export gas market selling into a tight Asian LNG market. The link between them is the domestic gas reservation from 2027.

04

Prices

  • Cheaper than 2025. New South Wales averaged about A$80/MWh so far this year, against A$108 in the same period of 2025.
  • Volatile month to month. October averaged about A$128/MWh; most other months ran A$60–85.
  • Batteries flatten the evening peak, which is where most of the price risk used to sit.

Source: AEMO aggregated price and demand data, NSW1; our averages.
