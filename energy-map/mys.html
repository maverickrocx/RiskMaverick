Malaysia

A coal and gas power system with a single buyer, and one of the world's largest LNG exporters through Petronas. In 2026 its new monthly fuel pass-through brought the Middle East shock into bills, and the government shielded most households.

SUMMARY

At a glance

  • Fuel costs reach bills monthly. The Automatic Fuel Adjustment passes fuel and currency changes into Peninsular tariffs each month (TNB).
  • Most households shielded. Users up to 800 kWh a month are exempt from the surcharge from September to December 2026.
  • Petronas sells more LNG. Gross LNG sales rose 17% to 20.3 million tonnes in the first half (Petronas).
  • Coal and gas dominate Peninsular generation.
  • Gas for power is regulated under tariffs running to the end of 2027.
The call

Malaysia earns from high LNG prices as an exporter and pays for them as a gas and coal burner. Policy decides how much of the cost reaches consumers.
