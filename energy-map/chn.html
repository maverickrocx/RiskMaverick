China

The world's largest power system and its biggest builder of solar and wind. In 2026 coal fell below half of China's output for the first time, and cheap domestic gas kept China out of the spot LNG scramble.

SUMMARY

At a glance

  • Coal below half. Coal supplied 49.7% of output in the first half of 2026, the first time under 50% (State Council).
  • Capacity keeps growing. 4,130 GW installed at end-September, up 11.2%; solar 1,310 GW and wind 700 GW (NEA).
  • Solar is now the largest source of capacity, overtaking coal (Bloomberg).
  • Plants run fewer hours. Average utilisation fell by 194 hours on a year earlier as capacity outgrew demand.
  • A swing LNG buyer. Weak domestic gas prices are curbing Chinese LNG buying into winter, freeing cargoes for Europe and other Asian buyers (Bloomberg).
The call

China matters to every other market on this map as the swing buyer of LNG. When its domestic gas is cheap and its solar fleet keeps growing, it buys less spot LNG and Europe and Japan pay less.

04

Prices

  • Regulated retail and wholesale prices dominate; provincial spot markets are growing but fragmented.
  • For other markets the key price is LNG: Chinese demand helps set JKM and, through it, TTF.

China publishes no single national wholesale power price.
