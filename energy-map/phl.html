Philippines

An island grid with a gross-pool spot market and a gas supply in transition from the declining Malampaya field to imported LNG. By mid-2026 spot prices were nearly double their level of late 2025.

SUMMARY

At a glance

  • Spot prices high. The WESM system-wide average was ₱8.31/kWh in July 2026, down from ₱9.54 in June but far above ₱4.54 in October 2025 (IEMOP).
  • LNG replaces Malampaya. Imported LNG at Batangas now supplies the gas plants that the domestic field once fed.
  • Three grids. Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao can price differently when links are constrained.
  • Coal is still the largest source of generation.
  • Exposed to LNG prices as a new importer in a tight market.
The call

Philippine spot power now prices off imported LNG and grid constraints. High LNG prices and thin reserves on Luzon are the combination to watch.
