## Business {#business}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Breaking News alerts and Businessweek Daily.*

**Hui Ka Yan, founder of Evergrande, once the world's biggest property developer by sales, was sentenced to life in prison by a Chinese court for "multiple combined crimes," including fraud and misuse of funds.** Evergrande inflated its asset values and concealed liabilities before defaulting on $300 billion of debt in 2021; the firm and a subsidiary were separately fined $2.4 billion.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/china-sentences-evergrande-founder-hui-ka-yan-to-life-in-prison)

**Target raised its full-year guidance after a second straight quarter of sales growth that beat Wall Street estimates, sending shares to their highest in almost two years, while Walmart posted its slowest US sales growth in six years on pharmacy pressure.** Target's turnaround chief credited gains in categories the retailer has been overhauling since a three-year slump; Walmart is responding by cutting prices across thousands of items.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/tgt-target-lifts-full-year-outlook-as-recovery-efforts-gain-ground) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/walmart-posts-sluggish-sales-with-slowest-us-growth-in-six-years)

## Economics {#economics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Markets Daily.*

**US federal debt formally surpassed $40 trillion for the first time, the Treasury confirmed, prompting Secretary Scott Bessent to raise long-term bond buybacks from $2 billion to at least $4 billion per operation.** The move pulled the 30-year Treasury yield back down to 5.19% after it had touched a 19-year high of 5.34% on investor concern over inflation and issuance.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/us-public-debt-hits-40-trillion-high-raising-doom-loop-risk) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/long-dated-treasuries-rally-as-treasury-boosts-bond-buybacks)

**Bessent is cementing his position as the most interventionist Treasury secretary in decades, putting his own credibility on the line as he tries to hold down yields, while a JPMorgan team warns the buyback plan risks being only a short-term circuit breaker.** Most respondents to Bloomberg's Markets Pulse survey still expect the 10-year yield to top 5% this year regardless.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/bessent-becomes-most-interventionist-treasury-chief-in-decades) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/jpmorgan-team-sees-credibility-risk-in-treasury-s-bond-buybacks)

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Markets Daily.*

— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/hudson-river-trading-posts-record-11-4-billion-trading-haul)

— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/dollar-risks-becoming-biggest-loser-from-bessent-s-bond-buying)
