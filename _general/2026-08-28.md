## Business {#business}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Evening Briefing Americas.*

**Alphabet has lost roughly $692 billion in market value since its May peak, with shares down about 15% as investor sentiment sours on heavy AI infrastructure spending and a delayed Gemini model launch.** The slide has made Alphabet a major drag on the S&P 500.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/alphabet-loses-692-billion-in-market-value-as-ai-questions-rise)

## Economics {#economics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Businessweek Daily.*

**Canada's current account surplus more than doubled economists' expectations in the second quarter as crude oil exports hit a record, giving Ottawa a trade cushion just as its tariff dispute with the US intensifies.** The cushion strengthens Canada's hand in the standoff.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/canada-current-account-surplus-hits-highest-since-2005-on-oil-surge)

## Finance {#finance}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Banking Monitor.*

**AI-powered mortgage lenders are cutting loan-approval times to a fraction of the usual process, a shift expected to accelerate refinancing and cut off income streams for investors in the $9 trillion mortgage-bond market sooner than they had priced in.** Only about a third of eligible homeowners currently refinance when rates fall, largely from friction that AI is now removing.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/ai-s-two-minute-home-refinancings-menace-mortgage-bond-returns)

## Politics {#politics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Evening Briefing Americas.*

**President Trump signed an executive order renaming Lake Ontario "Lake America" and his aides floated further bans on Canadian goods, as Canada responded by adding 50% tariffs on US copper wire, wood and charcoal.** The escalation comes despite a growing backlash within Trump's own party over the trade war's economic and midterm risks.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/trump-renames-lake-ontario-as-lake-america-stoking-canada-spat) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/canada-adds-50-tariffs-to-us-copper-wire-wood-charcoal)

## Tech / AI {#tech-ai}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Businessweek Daily.*

**Nvidia said quarterly sales more than doubled and forecast a further 89% jump to $108 billion this quarter, while disclosing $109 billion in loan guarantees — mostly for OpenAI's Ohio data center — and up to $56 billion in promised data-center payments regardless of usage.** Smaller customers accounted for 42% of the quarter's revenue, more than double their share a year earlier.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-08-27/nvidia-s-promises-more-growth-after-a-blockbuster-quarter)
