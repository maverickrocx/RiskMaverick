## Business {#business}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Morning Briefing Asia.*

**Alibaba is seeking to raise about HK$80 billion ($10.2 billion) from a share sale, offering 710 million shares at a 3.6% discount to Friday's close, to fund its AI expansion.** Investor Michael Burry criticised the move, saying he had already sold his Alibaba stake to buy a "large" position in JD.com instead.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/alibaba-to-raise-10-billion-by-selling-shares-for-ai-expansion)

## Finance {#finance}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Points of Return.*

**Bitcoin surged as much as 23% after the US Treasury pledged to at least double long-dated bond buybacks, reviving the "debasement trade," with Coinbase recording $1.72 billion in transactions between Wednesday and Thursday against a 30-day average of $367.5 million.** The rally marks the biggest daily gains for the token since February after a nearly 50% plunge from its October peak.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/newsletters/2026-08-24/canada-trade-war-an-elbow-in-the-face-may-get-us-attention)

## Politics {#politics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Morning Briefing Asia.*

**Canada will impose counter-tariffs on $20 billion of US goods including plywood, liquor, electronics and hockey sticks starting September 8th, matching the 50% US tariffs that took effect after trade talks between the two countries collapsed.** Prime Minister Mark Carney called the retaliation reluctant but framed it as one-for-one, calling his country "the best connected economy in the world" after signing more than 20 trade and security deals across five continents.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-22/canada-unveils-20-billion-counter-tariffs-to-mirror-trump-levy)

## Tech / AI {#tech-ai}

*Sourced from the Economic Times and Bloomberg's Morning Briefing Asia.*

**Anthropic's planned market debut could exceed the $85.7 billion raised by SpaceX's record IPO, with bankers telling potential investors it could seek more than $100 billion at a valuation near $2 trillion, US media reported.** SpaceX went public in June at a $1.77 trillion valuation.
— [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-market-debut-could-break-spacex-ipo-record/articleshow/133442327.cms)

**Some of Nvidia's biggest customers have been told that prices for servers containing its AI chips are rising more than 15%, with memory-chip costs soaring, as the increases take effect on systems shipped early next year including flagship Vera Rubin and Grace Blackwell chips.** The hikes add a fresh cost pressure to the AI infrastructure build-out.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-22/nvidia-customers-notified-about-ai-related-price-hikes-above-15)
