*Sourced from the Economist's Business in Brief.*

**China's producer prices rose more slowly than forecast in July.** The
producer-price index was up 3.5% year on year, down from 4.1% the month
before, according to the National Bureau of Statistics. Consumer prices rose
0.5% over the same period — the smallest increase since January. Lower
domestic oil prices helped curtail both measures.
