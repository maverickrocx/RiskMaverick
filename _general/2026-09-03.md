— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/india-s-rupee-gains-as-rbi-measures-bring-in-bumper-136-billion)

— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/yen-surges-with-traders-on-high-alert-for-further-intervention) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/boj-is-said-to-favor-quarter-point-hike-flexible-on-future-path)
