**Berkshire Hathaway's new boss is putting the money to work.** A disclosure
released on Saturday showed the investment firm ended the second quarter with
$365.5bn on hand, down about 8% from a record $397.4bn at the close of the
first quarter. Berkshire spent $4.5bn on a share buyback and $10bn adding to
its Alphabet holding, which has now entered its top five positions alongside
Apple, American Express, Bank of America and Coca-Cola. Across the quarter it
bought $23.5bn of equities against $3.7bn sold — its first quarter as a net
buyer of stocks in 15.
