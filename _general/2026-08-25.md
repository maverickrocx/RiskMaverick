## Business {#business}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Deals newsletter.*

**Shein is seeking to raise as much as HK$13.9 billion ($1.8 billion) in its long-awaited Hong Kong IPO, targeting a valuation of up to $27 billion, with investors covering the available shares on the first day of orders.** The listing caps an arduous, years-long path to going public for the fast-fashion retailer.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/shein-seeks-up-to-1-8-billion-in-long-awaited-hong-kong-ipo)

## Finance {#finance}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Deals newsletter.*

**India's equity capital market is on track for its best month on record, with roughly $10 billion raised in August, bucking a lackluster stock market as ample domestic liquidity keeps fueling demand for new share sales.** The rush stands in contrast to muted broader index performance this year.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/india-s-10-billion-equity-rush-puts-august-on-track-for-record)

## Politics {#politics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Evening Briefing Americas.*

**President Trump pledged to double US tariffs on Canadian autos to 50% and impose new levies on auto parts starting January 1st, escalating the trade war after negotiations collapsed last week; a University of Calgary economist estimates up to 90,000 Canadian jobs — about 0.4% of the labor force — could be lost if the tariffs persist.** Canada's dollar slipped against the US currency on the news.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/trump-vows-to-double-tariffs-on-canadian-autos-escalating-fight)

## Tech / AI {#tech-ai}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Money Stuff.*

**Anthropic is preparing to give CEO Dario Amodei and other co-founders a new class of stock carrying extra voting power to insulate them from outside shareholders, while its Long-Term Benefit Trust — now down to three members including former Federal Reserve Chair Ben Bernanke — separately retains the power to elect a majority of the company's board through non-economic shares.** The move would be the first time Anthropic's leaders have held outsized voting control.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/newsletters/2026-08-24/who-should-control-anthropic)
