**Bajaj Finance and Tata Capital fell after the RBI's latest draft norms
targeted their flexi-loan business.** Bajaj Finance dropped 5.84% on the
day after the central bank issued draft guidelines that would prohibit
NBFCs from offering a revolving credit facility — a category that includes
the flexi loans in which Bajaj Finance has the highest exposure among
lenders. The broader market closed out the week lower: the Sensex fell
0.58% to 78,499.17 and the Nifty declined 0.27% to 24,570.65, with
Financial Services the day's weakest sector (-1.48%) even as Auto (+1.84%)
and IT (+1.42%) outperformed.
