**Colombia swears in a Trump-aligned president.** Abelardo de la Espriella
takes office on Friday, ending four turbulent years under left-wing
predecessor Gustavo Petro. The ultra-conservative populist — who also runs
his own fashion line — has pledged to pump oil and gas production and
cooperate with Washington against drug-trafficking groups, and is holding
his inauguration outside the capital, Bogotá, to underline his
anti-establishment pitch.
