## Economics {#economics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Evening Briefing Americas and Asia, and The Economic Times.*

**The US trade deficit widened 24.4% in July to $88.6 billion, the largest since early 2025, driven by a surge in imports of computers and other technology equipment tied to the AI build-out.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/us-trade-gap-widens-to-largest-since-early-last-year-on-imports)

— [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/indicators/after-35-years-japans-jcra-upgrades-india-sovereign-rating-to-a-from-bbb/articleshow/133719290.cms)

**Norway's $2.3 trillion sovereign wealth fund proposed cutting government bonds to 50% of its fixed-income portfolio from 70%, a shift that implies a roughly $75 billion drop in its US Treasury holdings.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/norway-wealth-fund-proposes-trimming-government-bond-holdings)
