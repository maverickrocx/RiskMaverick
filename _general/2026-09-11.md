## Economics {#economics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Businessweek Daily and The Economist's World in Brief and Business in Brief.*

**US core consumer prices rose 0.3% in August, more than forecast, even as the annual core rate eased slightly to 2.4% from 2.5%; headline inflation held at 3.4%, keeping prices above the Federal Reserve's target and pushing traders' odds of a rate move next week above 85%, up from about 70% before the data.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/us-core-cpi-rises-more-than-forecast-bolstering-case-for-hike)

**The European Central Bank raised its deposit rate a quarter-point to 2.5% — its second hike this year and the highest level since April 2025 — as it tries to contain a bout of inflation driven by higher energy prices; European stocks fell and bond yields rose on expectations of further increases to come.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/ecb-to-hike-interest-rates-again-as-iran-war-fans-inflation)

## Finance {#finance}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Markets Daily.*

**A global bond selloff pushed the US 10-year Treasury yield to the cusp of 5% — near its highest since 2007 — after the Treasury's tripled buyback of long-dated debt fell short of the maximum size and failed to calm markets; the rout spread to Australian and Japanese yields as well.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/global-bond-selloff-sends-10-year-treasury-yields-to-cusp-of-5) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/treasury-buys-less-than-maximum-in-longer-debt-buyback-operation)
