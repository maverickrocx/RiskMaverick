## Business {#business}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's World in Brief and Evening Briefing Americas.*

**Evergrande founder Hui Ka Yan, once Asia's second-richest man, was sentenced to life in prison by a Chinese court for "multiple combined crimes" after pleading guilty in April to fraud and misuse of funds.** The former property giant inflated the value of its assets and concealed liabilities before defaulting on $300 billion of debt in 2021; it and a subsidiary were fined $2.4 billion.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/china-sentences-evergrande-founder-hui-ka-yan-to-life-in-prison)

**Beauty conglomerate Coty fell 7.6% even after its revenue beat expectations, after the company declined to give guidance and instead predicted a "transition" period this year.** The move underscores how skittish investors have become about companies withholding forward guidance.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/coty-s-revenue-beats-expectations-amid-turnaround-push)

## Economics {#economics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Markets Daily and Evening Briefing Americas.*

**US public debt surpassed $40 trillion for the first time, and the Treasury responded by boosting its long-term debt buybacks to at least $4 billion per operation after 30-year yields hit a 19-year high.** Wall Street's initial rally on the news fizzled by the next session, with 30-year yields climbing back as investors judged the move a short-term fix rather than a solution to the underlying borrowing problem.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/us-public-debt-hits-40-trillion-high-raising-doom-loop-risk) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/bessent-says-buybacks-could-be-more-than-4-billion-per-issue)

**Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is cementing his position as the most interventionist Treasury chief in decades, putting his own credibility on the line to keep a lid on yields.** Most respondents to Bloomberg's latest Markets Pulse survey still expect the 10-year Treasury yield to top 5% before the year is out.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/bessent-becomes-most-interventionist-treasury-chief-in-decades)

— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/bitcoin-btc-roars-past-70-000-as-yields-sink-trump-cour)

**Quant firm Hudson River Trading posted $11.4 billion in trading revenue and $7.4 billion in net profit for the second quarter, as Iran-war volatility and swings in AI stocks created rich opportunities to trade.** The result contrasts with a rougher July for peers: Jane Street reported a $15 billion loss last month, partly tied to the collapse of hedge fund Situational Awareness.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/hudson-river-trading-posts-record-11-4-billion-trading-haul)

## Politics {#politics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Evening Briefing Americas and Morning Briefing Asia.*

**China agreed to eliminate almost all tariffs on Swiss goods under a new trade accord, granting tariff-free treatment to 99.8% of Switzerland's exports as Beijing looks to deepen European ties even as Trump wages trade wars against traditional US partners.** The deal offers Switzerland relief after Trump initially hit it with a 39% tariff last summer, though a preliminary US-Swiss agreement capping tariffs at 15% remains unsigned.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/swiss-agree-trade-deal-that-gives-it-tariff-free-access-to-china)

**North Korea fired about ten ballistic missiles, its third launch this month, shortly after Pyongyang cast doubt on Trump's claim that high-level talks between the two governments had been revived.** The launch underscores the challenges facing Trump's efforts to restart personal diplomacy with Kim Jong Un.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/korea-s-lee-hails-trump-s-determination-for-peninsula-peace)

**Anthropic expects to match or exceed the size of SpaceX's record-setting $75 billion initial public offering as preparations for the AI company's debut pick up speed, people familiar with the matter said.** The deal would rank among the largest IPOs on record if it proceeds at that scale.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/anthropic-expects-to-match-spacex-s-record-ipo-size-or-top-it)

— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/broadcom-seeks-more-than-60-billion-in-latest-ai-debt-deal)
