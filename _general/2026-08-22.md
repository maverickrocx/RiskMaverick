## Business {#business}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Evening Briefing Asia and Tech In Depth.*

**Samsung plans to return as much as $79 billion to shareholders while SK Hynix's net cash is estimated to have topped 100 trillion won ($68 billion), as Korea's memory giants face intensifying pressure to share windfall AI-boom profits.** Both companies are simultaneously ramping capital expenditure — SK Hynix by 50% to at least $31 billion and Samsung above $73 billion — leaving little cushion if AI spending slows.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/samsung-plans-as-much-as-79-billion-in-shareholder-returns)

**Alibaba's revenue rose 9% year-on-year to 269 billion yuan ($40 billion) in the second quarter, but profit fell 75% to 10.5 billion yuan as AI-related costs mounted and capital expenditure jumped 75% to almost $10 billion.** Investors were unnerved, sending the Chinese tech firm's American-listed shares down more than 4% on the news.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/alibaba-s-revenue-climbs-9-in-testament-to-china-s-ai-boom)

## Economics {#economics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Odd Lots and Evening Briefing Asia.*

**Fed Chair Kevin Warsh heads into his first Jackson Hole symposium next week as the Treasury's surprise bond-buyback expansion complicates his push for less forward guidance, with three FOMC members having dissented at the July meeting.** Minutes released this week showed considerable uncertainty about the inflation path, while regional presidents Jeffrey Schmid and Alberto Musalem have since signaled they favor a hike.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-08-21/what-to-look-out-for-at-the-fed-s-jackson-hole-gathering)

**China is doubling down on a targeted fiscal program to drive borrowing by businesses and consumers, with new measures set for launch later this year as growth veers below the government's annual target.** Data this week showed industrial output, consumption and investment all softened more than expected in July.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/china-plans-new-fiscal-support-policies-this-year-amid-slowdown)

## Finance {#finance}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Markets Daily, Evening Briefing Americas and Going Private.*

**Gold is on track for a third straight weekly gain and the dollar is trading at a three-month low after the US Treasury's surprise pledge to at least double long-dated bond buybacks, reviving "debasement trade" chatter.** Robin Brooks of the Brookings Institution called the move the "clearest sign yet" the US is following Japan toward currency debasement, warning the administration is "playing with fire."
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/gold-set-for-third-weekly-gain-on-us-treasury-buyback-plans) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/us-buyback-pledge-draws-japan-comparisons-and-pressures-dollar)

**Broadcom is in talks with lenders including Apollo and Blackstone to raise more than $60 billion in debt for an AI chip financing deal that will benefit Anthropic and others, with the total raise potentially reaching $100 billion.** The financing underscores the scale of capital now flowing into AI infrastructure buildouts.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/broadcom-seeks-more-than-60-billion-in-latest-ai-debt-deal)
