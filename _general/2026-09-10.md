## Business {#business}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Evening Briefing Americas and Energy Daily, and The Economist's Business in Brief.*

## Economics {#economics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Morning Briefing Asia and Markets Daily, and The Economist's World in Brief.*

**The US Treasury said it will increase its next buyback of long-dated debt to $6 billion, but the move failed to calm markets: investors had expected a bigger programme and 10-year yields rose as much as 6 basis points to 4.85%, the highest since 2023. Evercore ISI said the step suggests Scott Bessent is "accepting a limited role for buybacks."**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/us-more-than-doubles-long-dated-debt-buyback-size-to-6-billion)

**The European Central Bank is expected to raise its deposit rate by a quarter-point to 2.5% on Thursday — its second hike since the Iran war sent energy prices soaring — with all but one economist in a Bloomberg poll expecting the move as inflation stays above target and euro-area growth holds up.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/ecb-to-hike-interest-rates-again-as-iran-war-fans-inflation) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/traders-bet-on-four-rate-hikes-from-ecb-and-boe-as-oil-surges)

## Finance {#finance}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Morning Briefing Asia and India Edition.*

**US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent publicly dared traders to bet against a stronger yen, telling them "I am the house now" — an unusually direct verbal intervention after the currency's sharp rally through ¥155 per dollar.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-08/bessent-dares-traders-to-bet-against-yen-i-am-the-house-now)

**Foreign investors have pulled more than $1 billion from Indian equities in September, taking the year-to-date outflow past $25 billion even as growth and earnings data improve; the Nifty 50 has now traded below its 200-day moving average for 130 days, its longest such run in nearly a decade.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-09-09/why-foreign-investors-still-aren-t-buying-indian-stocks)
