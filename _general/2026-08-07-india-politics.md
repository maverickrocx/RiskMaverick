**Parliament has cleared the way for UPI to stop being free for merchants.**
The Lok Sabha passed the Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026,
which opens the door to a merchant discount rate (MDR) on UPI transactions —
still under discussion, but a fee of 25-30 basis points on transactions
above ₹2,000 is the figure being floated, well below the 80-90bp on debit
cards or 250bp on credit cards. Banks would take the largest share of the
revenue (60-65%), with the rest split between merchant acquirers like Paytm
and PhonePe, PSP banks and NPCI. At 25bp, the fee could raise roughly
₹13,000 crore a year.
