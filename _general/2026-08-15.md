## Business {#business}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Morning Briefing Americas.*

**Americans are dipping into savings to fund their hobbies.** Spending on
pastimes — needlework, scrapbooking, marathon running and the rest — has
reached a record share of US goods consumption, running above even pandemic-era
highs. Amazon and Michaels are among the beneficiaries.

## Economics {#economics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Morning Briefing Americas.*

**Fitch kept America at AA+ but flagged how exposed it is to a shock.** The
agency warned that high fiscal deficits leave the country vulnerable, noting
that government debt is more than double the median for similarly rated
nations. The affirmation lands the same week the Treasury went to market with a
30-year bond auction at the highest yield since 2001, and after a July budget
deficit swollen by accelerating spending.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/us-braces-for-30-year-bond-auction-at-highest-yield-since-2001) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/us-budget-deficit-surged-in-july-on-acceleration-in-spending)

## Finance {#finance}

*Sourced from The Economist's Business in Brief.*

**The firms China relies on to defuse its credit crises are themselves in
trouble.** The Economist examines the country's asset-management companies —
the "debt-bomb squad" set up to absorb bad loans from the banking system — and
finds the cleanup vehicles are now carrying problems of their own.
— [The Economist](https://www.economist.com/finance-and-economics/2026/08/13/is-chinas-debt-bomb-squad-about-to-blow-up)

## Politics {#politics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Morning Briefing Americas.*

**Washington has put tariffs of up to 100% on imported drones and their
components.** The measure is framed as reducing reliance on foreign suppliers
and the announcement did not mention China by name, but it lands squarely on
the world's dominant drone manufacturer and risks reinforcing a two-way
decoupling already under way.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/trump-imposes-100-tariffs-on-certain-drones-countering-china)

**The "golden age" pitch is not landing with voters.** With midterms less than
three months away, President Trump's argument that the economic good times have
already arrived is running into public sentiment that says otherwise: approval
sits at 39% per RealClearPolitics, and inflation is back above 3% even as
unemployment stays low and consumer spending holds up. Some Republicans want
fresh economic proposals, including further tax cuts.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/trump-s-economy-pitch-falls-flat-with-voters-ahead-of-midterms) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/trump-weighs-call-for-capital-gains-tax-cuts-as-midterm-boost)

## Tech / AI {#tech-ai}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Morning Briefing Americas.*

**OpenAI's annualised revenue has passed $40 billion, roughly double its run
rate at the end of 2025.** People familiar with the figures attribute the
acceleration to subscription sales and a nascent advertising business. The
company filed confidentially for an initial public offering in June, and the
revenue trajectory strengthens that case.
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/openai-s-revenue-run-rate-tops-40-billion-ahead-of-ipo) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-08/openai-filed-confidentially-for-ipo-as-rivals-race-to-market)
