## Economics {#economics}

*Sourced from Bloomberg's Evening Briefing Asia and Markets Daily, and The Economist.*

**A deepening global bond selloff pushed 30-year Treasury yields to about 5.28% — a 19-year high that wiped out the drop after Scott Bessent expanded debt buybacks — while Japan's 10-year yield hit 3% for the first time since 1996 and German bunds reached 3.39%.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/bessent-s-bond-gains-wiped-out-as-30-year-yields-jump-once-again) · [The Economist](https://www.economist.com/finance-and-economics/2026/09/01/europes-bond-markets-are-suffering-a-post-holiday-shock)

**Traders now price a September rate hike at better than even odds for three major central banks — around 70% for the Fed — after New Zealand delivered back-to-back increases to 2.75% and the ECB's Joachim Nagel signalled a hike next week.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/new-zealand-delivers-back-to-back-rate-hikes-to-curb-inflation) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/nagel-signals-ecb-will-hike-next-week-cautious-beyond-that)

**The G20 finance ministers' meeting in Asheville ended without a joint communique after China alone rejected language criticising "excessive and persistent external surpluses"; the other 19 members backed a chair's statement.**
— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/bessent-says-china-prevented-g20-communique-over-imbalance-spat)
